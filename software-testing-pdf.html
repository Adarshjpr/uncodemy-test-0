Roadmap for Software Testing

A software testing roadmap provides beginners as well as professionals with a step-by-step manual for learning testing from the ground up. Usually, it consisted of:

  • Understanding the Basics Understand about the software development lifecycle (SDLC) and the many types of testing, such as controlled automatically functional, ineffective, and manual testing.
  • Proficiency in Manual Testing: Acknowledge basic ideas concerning creating test cases, reporting errors, and executing them.
  • Instruments for Computerized Testing: Get practical experience with TestNG, JUnit, and Selenium.
  • Use performance and security testing to determine your system's speed, scalability, and weaknesses in security.
  • As part of your continuing education, look at revolutionary technologies, CI/CD integration, and agile testing approaches.

Following the appropriate study plan ensures a methodical approach to learning and helps students become experienced testers.
