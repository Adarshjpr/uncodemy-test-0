AI Basic

Top AI Features You Should Know

Natural Language Understanding

Modern AI models can understand and generate human language, enabling chatbots, summarization tools, and translation services.

Computer Vision

AI can analyze images and video to detect objects, recognize faces, and understand scenes — powering applications from security to medical imaging.

Predictive Analytics

Machine learning models can forecast trends, such as sales, demand, or equipment failure, based on historical data patterns.

Automation & Agentic Workflows

AI agents can complete multi-step tasks autonomously, such as scheduling, data entry, and customer support resolution.

Personalization

Recommendation engines use AI to tailor content, products, and experiences to individual users based on behavior.

Generative Capabilities

Generative AI can create text, images, audio, and code, opening new possibilities in content creation and software development.

Key Takeaway: Mastering this topic is a key step toward becoming a well-rounded AI professional, capable of building real-world, intelligent systems.
