Coupons and Discounts

Encouraging Purchases with Discounts

Coupons and discounts are a proven way to encourage first-time purchases, reward loyal customers, and clear out seasonal stock in a WooCommerce store.

Creating a Coupon in WooCommerce

Go to Marketing > Coupons > Add Coupon to set a unique coupon code that customers will enter at checkout.

Choose a discount type, percentage, fixed cart discount, or fixed product discount, and set the discount amount.

Setting Usage Restrictions

Add restrictions such as a minimum spend, expiry date, or limit on how many times a coupon can be used.

Restrict coupons to specific products or categories if you want to promote particular items.

Discount TypeExampleBest Used For
Percentage discount10% offSitewide or seasonal sales
Fixed cart discount$5 off cartEncouraging larger orders
Fixed product discount$2 off one itemPromoting a specific product

As your store starts processing real orders and customer data, protecting it with strong security practices becomes essential.
