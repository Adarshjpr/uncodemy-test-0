What is WordPress? (org vs. com, Features)

What Does WordPress Actually Mean?

WordPress is the world's most popular content management system (CMS), powering a huge share of websites on the internet. It began as a simple blogging tool but has grown into a full platform capable of running blogs, business sites, portfolios, and online stores.

When people say "WordPress," they are usually referring to one of two very different things: WordPress.org (the self-hosted, open-source software) or WordPress.com (a hosted service built on top of that same software). Understanding the difference is the first step before you build anything.

WordPress.org vs. WordPress.com

WordPress.org is free, open-source software that you download and install on your own web hosting account. You get full control over themes, plugins, and the site's code, but you are also responsible for hosting, backups, and updates.

WordPress.com is a hosted service run by Automattic. It handles hosting and maintenance for you, but free and lower-tier plans restrict custom themes, plugins, and monetisation options.

AspectWordPress.orgWordPress.com
HostingYou choose your own hostHosted by Automattic
CostSoftware is free; hosting/domain cost extraFree plan available; paid plans for more features
Themes & PluginsUnlimited, install any theme/pluginLimited on free/lower plans
ControlFull control over code and dataLimited control, platform-managed
Best ForBusinesses, developers, custom projectsBeginners wanting a quick, simple blog

Why WordPress Dominates the Web

WordPress powers a huge portion of all websites because it balances ease of use with deep flexibility. Whether you need a simple blog or a complex e-commerce store, WordPress can scale to fit the job.

Its open-source nature means a massive global community constantly builds free and premium themes, plugins, and tutorials, which keeps the platform evolving quickly.

Key Features That Make It Popular

  • A beginner-friendly dashboard lets non-technical users publish content without touching code.
  • Thousands of themes let you change a site's entire look in minutes, while page builders add drag-and-drop design control.
  • Plugins extend functionality for SEO, security, forms, e-commerce, and almost anything else you can imagine.
  • Built-in SEO-friendly markup and clean permalink options help pages rank better in search engines.

Core Benefits at a Glance

Beyond individual features, a few broader benefits explain why WordPress remains the default choice for so many projects.
