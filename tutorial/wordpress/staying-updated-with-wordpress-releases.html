Staying Updated with WordPress Releases

Keeping Your Skills Current

WordPress releases new versions regularly, each bringing new features, performance improvements, and occasional breaking changes worth tracking.

Where to Follow Releases

Follow the official WordPress News blog and the Make WordPress Core blog for release announcements and technical details.

Subscribe to newsletters or podcasts from trusted WordPress educators to get digestible summaries of major updates.

Staying Ready for New Features

Test new WordPress versions on a staging site before updating your live site, especially after major releases.

Periodically review your themes and plugins to ensure they remain compatible with the latest WordPress version.
