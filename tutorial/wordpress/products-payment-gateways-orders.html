Products, Payment Gateways & Orders

Running the Core of Your Store

Products, payment gateways, and order management form the operational heart of any WooCommerce store.

Adding and Managing Products

Go to Products > Add New to enter a product's title, description, price, images, and category.

Choose a product type, such as simple, variable (with size/color options), or virtual/downloadable, depending on what you sell.

Payment Gateways and Orders

Enable payment gateways like PayPal, Stripe, or direct bank transfer under WooCommerce > Settings > Payments.

View and manage incoming orders under WooCommerce > Orders, updating their status as you process, ship, and complete them.

TaskWhere to Manage It
Add a productProducts > Add New
Set product typeProduct data panel
Enable paymentsWooCommerce > Settings > Payments
Process an orderWooCommerce > Orders

With products and payments running smoothly, coupons and discounts are a simple next step to help drive more sales.
