Interview Prep

Top 30 Jira Interview Questions

Whether you're applying for a project coordinator, Scrum master, or Jira administrator role, interviewers often test both conceptual understanding and hands-on familiarity with Jira. This guide highlights the areas most commonly covered.

Core Topics Interviewers Ask About

Expect questions on the basics, such as the difference between an Epic and a Story, how workflows and transitions work, and the difference between Scrum and Kanban boards. Interviewers use these questions to check whether you understand Jira's fundamental structure, not just its interface.

Preparing Practical Examples

Beyond definitions, interviewers often ask candidates to describe a workflow they configured, a JQL query they wrote, or an automation rule they built. Preparing specific, real examples from practice projects makes your answers far stronger than general explanations.

Question Categories to Prepare For

  • Jira fundamentals: issues, projects, and workflows
  • Agile concepts: Scrum vs Kanban, sprints, backlogs
  • JQL and filters for searching and reporting
  • Automation rules and integration scenarios
  • Administration: permissions, roles, and user management
