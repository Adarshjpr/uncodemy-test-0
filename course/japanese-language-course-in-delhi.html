Beginners Level (JLPT N5)
  • Chapters: Introduction to Hiragana, Katakana, and basic Kanji.
  • Grammar Modules: Sentence structures, particles, present and past tense verbs.
  • Units: Greetings, numbers, time expressions, and simple conversations.
  • Topics Covered: Self-introduction, asking questions, shopping, and directions.
  • Books: Minna no Nihongo I, Genki I.
Intermediate Level (JLPT N4)
  • Grammar: Complex sentence structures, conjunctions, honorifics, and passive forms.
  • Kanji & Vocabulary: Around 300-500 Kanji characters and 1,500 vocabulary words.
  • Modules: Understanding spoken Japanese in casual and formal scenarios.
  • Topics: Travel conversations, cultural activities, and workplace interactions.
  • Books: Minna no Nihongo II, Genki II.
Advanced Level (JLPT N3, N2, and N1)
  • Grammar & Syntax: Advanced verb forms, idiomatic expressions, and nuanced speech.
  • Kanji & Vocabulary: Over 1,000 Kanji characters and 6,000+ words.
  • Specialized Units: Academic Japanese, business Japanese, and cultural texts.
  • Topics: Essay writing, business presentations, and professional email etiquette.
  • Books: Tobira, An Integrated Approach to Intermediate Japanese.
