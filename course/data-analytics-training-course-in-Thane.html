Why Data Analytics Course? What Will You Get?

Data Analytics is all about transforming raw data into valuable insights using various tools and techniques. It’s essential for identifying trends and solving problems, helping businesses make informed decisions. Here’s how the process works:

  • Set your goals.
  • Understand the business and the data context.
  • Get an overview of the data.
  • Formulate specific hypotheses.
  • Test those hypotheses.
  • Validate the results.

There are four main types of Data Analytics:

  • Descriptive: What happened?
  • Diagnostic: Why did it happen?
  • Predictive: What is likely to happen?
  • Prescriptive: What should we do about it?
