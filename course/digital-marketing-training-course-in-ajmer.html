What is Digital Marketing? Why you should do it from Uncodemy?

Digital marketing, or online marketing, is the strategic promotion of brands to connect with potential customers using the internet and various forms of digital communication. This includes everything from email and social media to web-based advertising and mobile messages. Its primary power lies in its measurability and data-driven nature, allowing marketers to track and optimize every customer interaction.
