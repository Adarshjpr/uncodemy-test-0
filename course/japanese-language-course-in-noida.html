Set the Basics Right

Our Japanese Language Course in Noida offers students all the essential skills needed to excel in the language. The well-organized curriculum covers everything, from basic grammar to advanced conversation, blending theory with hands-on practice.

Topics and Units Covered

Our curriculum is divided into units and levels based on CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) standards:

Introduction to Japanese Language and Culture

1. Introduction to the Japanese Language
  • Overview of Hiragana, Katakana, and Kanji writing systems
  • History of the Japanese language and its regional dialects
  • Key differences between Japanese and other languages
  • Importance of context in the Japanese language
2. Japanese Culture and Etiquette
  • Proper ways to greet and bow in different settings
  • Cultural norms in public spaces (transport, restaurants, etc.)
  • Basic phrases for politeness (sumimasen, arigatou, etc.)
  • Japanese festivals and traditions (e.g., Hanami, Obon, New Year)

Japanese Writing Systems

1. Hiragana
  • Detailed study of all 46 characters
  • Combination sounds (e.g., kya, kyu, kyo)
  • Practice reading and writing simple sentences
  • Memorization techniques and quizzes
2. Katakana
  • Study of 46 Katakana characters and their variations
  • Usage of Katakana for loanwords and emphasis
  • Translating English names and words into Katakana
  • Exercises to build fluency in Katakana
3. Basic Kanji
  • Introduction to radicals (components of Kanji)
  • Learning 50-100 foundational Kanji characters
  • How to write Kanji with proper stroke order
  • Using Kanji in basic sentences and phrases

Basic Grammar and Sentence Structure

1. Sentence Formation
  • Basic sentence patterns: affirmative, negative, and question forms
  • Use of subject, object, and topic markers
  • Differences between は (wa) and が (ga)
2. Verbs and Conjugation
  • Present, past, and negative conjugations
  • Polite and informal verb forms
  • Introduction to the Te-form and its uses
3. Adjectives and Their Usage
  • Types of adjectives (i-adjectives, na-adjectives)
  • Conjugating adjectives in present, past, and negative forms
  • Combining adjectives and nouns
4. Particles
  • Overview of essential particles (に, へ, で, を, と)
  • Functions of particles in sentences
  • Practicing sentence variations using particles

Building Vocabulary

1. Daily Life Vocabulary
  • Numbers, dates, and time-related words
  • Common words for family members, places, and activities
  • Words for describing everyday routines
2. Thematic Vocabulary
  • Travel-related vocabulary (e.g., airport, hotel, directions)
  • Shopping vocabulary (e.g., prices, items, bargaining phrases)
  • Food and dining vocabulary (e.g., menu items, table manners)
3. Advanced Vocabulary Techniques
  • Building vocabulary through flashcards and mnemonics
  • Grouping words into categories for better retention

Speaking and Listening Skills

1. Pronunciation and Intonation
  • Correct pronunciation of vowels, consonants, and combinations
  • Identifying and mimicking native intonation patterns
  • Correct use of pitch accents
2. Conversational Practice
  • Role-playing daily conversations (e.g., at a store, meeting someone)
  • Introducing oneself in formal and informal settings
  • Building confidence in speaking through repetition
3. Listening Comprehension
  • Understanding spoken Japanese through dialogues and podcasts
  • Recognizing key phrases and context in spoken language
  • Strategies for listening to fast-paced conversations

Reading and Writing Practice

1. Reading Practice
  • Reading short stories, emails, and notices
  • Understanding context and sentence flow
  • Gradually advancing to articles and excerpts from books
2. Writing Practice
  • Writing personal introductions, letters, and emails
  • Crafting sentences with correct grammar and vocabulary
  • Writing short essays on familiar topics
3. Kanji Application
  • Using Kanji in real-world contexts (signboards, instructions)
  • Exercises to reinforce Kanji knowledge

Intermediate Grammar and Vocabulary

1. Grammar Expansion
  • Expressing desires and preferences (たい, ほしい)
  • Understanding conditional sentences (と, たら, なら, ば)
  • Making comparisons and expressing opinions
2. Advanced Verbs and Conjugations
  • Using potential and passive forms of verbs
  • Understanding causative forms and their applications
  • Polite commands and requests
3. Intermediate Vocabulary
  • Vocabulary for formal situations (business and official terms)
  • Specialized words for hobbies, education, and technology

Advanced Topics and Proficiency Preparation

1. Advanced Grammar
  • Mastering keigo (敬語) for formal speech
  • Combining multiple clauses in sentences
  • Using idiomatic expressions and proverbs
2. Reading and Comprehension
  • Reading intermediate-level texts (news articles, short novels)
  • Strategies for deducing the meaning of unfamiliar words
3. Listening and Speaking
  • Reading intermediate-level texts (news articles, short novels)
  • Strategies for deducing the meaning of unfamiliar words

Exam Preparation (JLPT)

1. JLPT Levels N5 to N1 Overview
  • Key differences in difficulty levels
  • Grammar, vocabulary, and Kanji requirements for each level
2. Practice Sessions
  • Solving previous JLPT papers
  • Time management strategies for exams
3. Listening and Reading for JLPT
  • Focused exercises to improve comprehension speed

Career Guidance and Real-Life Applications

1. Using Japanese Professionally
  • Writing resumes and cover letters in Japanese
  • Preparing for Japanese job interviews
2. Real-Life Applications
  • Using Japanese in travel, education, and work settings
  • Networking with Japanese speakers
3. Advanced Networking and Freelancing
  • Building a portfolio with Japanese language skills
  • Finding opportunities for translation, teaching, or corporate roles
