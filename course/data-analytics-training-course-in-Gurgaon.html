    12. What does a data analyst do?

    A data analyst collects, organizes, and examines data to find useful insights. They help businesses make decisions based on data patterns. They use tools like Excel, SQL, and Power BI to analyze and visualize data.

    13. What is a data analyst?

    A data analyst is a professional who works with data to uncover trends, answer questions, and support business decisions. They use statistical methods and visualization tools to make data easy to understand.

    14. Who is a data analyst?

    A data analyst is someone skilled in working with numbers and databases. They help companies understand customer behavior, sales trends, and operational performance using data.

    15. Can a data analyst become a data scientist?

    Yes, a data analyst can become a data scientist by learning programming (Python/R), machine learning, and advanced statistics. Data science requires deeper technical knowledge compared to data analysis.

    16. Who can become a data analyst?

    Anyone with a strong interest in numbers, problem-solving, and technology can become a data analyst. A background in mathematics, computer science, or business analytics is helpful but not mandatory.

    17. Will data analysts be replaced by AI?

    AI can automate some data analysis tasks, but human analysts are still needed for decision-making, understanding business needs, and storytelling with data. AI will assist, not replace, data analysts.

    18. Which skills are required for a data analyst?

    A data analyst should know SQL, Excel, Power BI/Tableau, statistics, data visualization, and problem-solving. Good communication skills are also important to explain insights clearly.

    19. Can a data analyst work from home?

    Yes, many data analyst jobs can be done remotely. Analysts work on a computer, analyzing data and creating reports, which can be done from anywhere with an internet connection.

    20. Why become a data analyst?

    A data analyst career is rewarding, in demand, and offers good salaries. It allows problem-solving and helps businesses grow through data-driven decisions.

    21. How does a data analyst work?
