    Curriculum For Salesforce Development Training Course

    A well-structured Salesforce Development training course curriculum typically covers the essential tools, techniques, and concepts required for building and customizing applications on the Salesforce Platform. The course can be broken down into the following key components: ...
    • Introduction to Salesforce and Cloud Computing: Overview of Salesforce, CRM fundamentals, multi-tenant architecture, and the role of a developer.
    • Salesforce Fundamentals: Objects, fields, relationships, data modeling, standard vs. custom objects.
    • Declarative Customization: Validation rules, formulas, workflows, Process Builder, Flows, approval processes.
    • Apex Programming: Variables, data types, control statements, classes, interfaces, triggers, DML, SOQL, SOSL, governor limits, exception handling.
    • Visualforce and Controllers: Pages, standard/custom controllers, extensions, incorporating Visualforce in Lightning.
    • Lightning Web Components (LWC): Modern UI development, components, events, wiring data, security in LWC.
    • Integration and APIs: REST/SOAP APIs, external services, MuleSoft basics, connected apps.
    • Testing and Debugging: Unit testing, test classes, debug logs, Developer Console, deployment best practices.
    • Security and Sharing: Profiles, roles, permission sets, sharing rules, OWASP considerations.
    • Deployment and Tools: Change sets, packages, CI/CD basics, environments (sandbox vs. production).
    • AI and Advanced Topics: Agentforce, Einstein integration, ethical AI in Salesforce.
    • Capstone Projects: Real-world end-to-end projects applying declarative and programmatic development.
    • Ethics, Governance, and Best Practices: Responsible development, compliance, and platform optimization.
