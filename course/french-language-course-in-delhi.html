French Language Course Curriculum in Delhi

The French language course curriculum at Uncodemy is divided into several modules, each focusing on different aspects of the language. Here's a breakdown of the topics and units covered:

Here’s what you can expect from our French course:

Module 1: Introduction to French Language and Culture

Introduction to the French Language
  • Overview of the French alphabet, pronunciation, and accents
  • History of the French language and its global influence
  • Key differences between French and other languages
  • The role of gender, formalities, and context in French
French Culture and Etiquette
  • Proper ways to greet and address people in various settings
  • Cultural norms for dining, transportation, and social interactions
  • Common phrases for politeness (e.g., merci, excusez-moi)
  • French festivals and traditions (e.g., Bastille Day, Christmas, Easter)

Module 2: French Pronunciation and Writing Basics

French Alphabet and Sounds
  • Mastering vowel and consonant combinations
  • Pronunciation rules for silent letters and nasal sounds
  • Practice reading and writing simple words and phrases
Introduction to Basic Writing
  • Writing common phrases and sentences
  • Understanding punctuation and capitalization in French
  • Exercises for developing writing fluency

Module 3: Basic Grammar and Sentence Structure

Sentence Formation
  • Basic sentence structures: affirmative, negative, and interrogative
  • Use of subject pronouns and agreement rules
  • Introduction to être (to be) and avoir (to have) verbs
Verbs and Conjugation
  • Present tense conjugations for regular -er, -ir, and -re verbs
  • Introduction to irregular verbs (e.g., aller, faire, venir)
  • Building sentences with action verbs
Nouns, Adjectives, and Articles
  • Gender and number agreement
  • Types of articles (definite, indefinite, partitive)
  • Using adjectives in descriptive sentences
Essential Grammar
  • Introduction to prepositions (à, de, avec, etc.)
  • Forming simple and complex phrases

Module 4: Vocabulary Building

Daily Life Vocabulary
  • Numbers, days of the week, months, and telling time
  • Common words for family, places, and daily routines
  • Essential phrases for everyday use
Thematic Vocabulary
  • Travel-related vocabulary (e.g., airports, hotels, directions)
  • Shopping vocabulary (e.g., items, prices, common phrases)
  • Dining and food vocabulary (e.g., menus, ordering, table etiquette)
Memory Techniques
  • Using flashcards, mnemonics, and word groups for retention

Module 5: Speaking and Listening Skills

Pronunciation Practice
  • Mastering French sounds, intonation, and rhythm
  • Differentiating homophones and practicing pitch
Conversational Practice
  • Role-playing common scenarios (e.g., introductions, shopping, traveling)
  • Building confidence through interactive speaking exercises
Listening Comprehension
  • Understanding spoken French through audio clips, podcasts, and dialogues
  • Recognizing key phrases and grasping context in conversations

Module 6: Reading and Writing Skills

Reading Practice
  • Reading short texts, emails, and everyday materials
  • Developing skills to understand context and main ideas
Writing Practice
  • Writing personal introductions, emails, and short essays
  • Using appropriate grammar and vocabulary in writing

Module 7: Intermediate Grammar and Vocabulary

Grammar Expansion
  • Expressing opinions, comparisons, and preferences
  • Introduction to conditional sentences (si, quand, etc.)
Advanced Verbs and Conjugation
  • Mastering future and past tenses (passé composé, imparfait)
  • Using reflexive verbs and passive forms
Intermediate Vocabulary
  • Words for professional settings (business, education)
  • Vocabulary for hobbies, arts, and travel

Module 8: Advanced Topics and Proficiency Preparation

Advanced Grammar
  • Using subjunctive mood and relative clauses
  • Idiomatic expressions and proverbs
Advanced Reading and Comprehension
  • Reading news articles, short stories, and French literature excerpts
Speaking in Real-Life Scenarios
  • Role-playing professional and formal situations (e.g., interviews)
  • Simulating business meetings and public speaking

Module 9: Exam Preparation (DELF/DALF)

Exam Overview
  • Understanding DELF and DALF exam levels (A1 to C2)
  • Key requirements for grammar, vocabulary, and comprehension
Practice Sessions
  • Solving mock papers and time management strategies
Listening and Reading Practice
  • Exercises focused on improving accuracy and speed

Module 10: Career Guidance and Real-Life Applications

Using French Professionally
  • Writing resumes and cover letters in French
  • Preparing for French-speaking job interviews
Real-Life Applications
  • Using French for travel, networking, and daily interactions
  • Exploring opportunities in teaching, translation, and corporate roles
