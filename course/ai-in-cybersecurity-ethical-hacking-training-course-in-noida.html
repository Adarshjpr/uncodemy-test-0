Why Ethical Hacking Matters?

You may wonder, “Why should someone learn hacking if hacking is illegal?” The answer is simple: Ethical Hacking is the legal and responsible side of hacking.

  • Ethical Hackers work for companies to find system weaknesses before bad hackers do.
  • They use penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, and other tools to test the strength of firewalls, apps, and networks.
  • Their role is to think like a hacker but act like a protector.

So, when you combine AI + Ethical Hacking, you become a super-skilled professional who can stop cyberattacks even before they happen.
