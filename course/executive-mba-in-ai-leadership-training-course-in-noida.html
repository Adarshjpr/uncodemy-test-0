One of the highlights of the Executive MBA in AI Leadership (12 Months) at Uncodemy in Noida is the Capstone Project. This is where you take all the knowledge from the classroom and apply it to solve a real-world business problem. Unlike simple assignments, a capstone is industry-grade and designed to make you job-ready.

What is a Capstone Project?
• It is a final project you will work on during the last stage of the program.
• It allows you to use AI technologies + leadership skills + business knowledge together.
• You will work individually or in teams, depending on the project.
• Mentors from Fortune 500 companies and Uncodemy trainers will guide you.
• You will present the final project to a panel of experts, just like a boardroom presentation.
