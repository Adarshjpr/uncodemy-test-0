What Is Autocad? Why You Should Do It From Uncodemy?

Autocad is a computer-aided design (CAD) software application that is widely used in the architecture, engineering, and construction industries. It was first released in 1982 by Autodesk and has since become the industry standard for 2D and 3D drafting and modeling.

Autocad allows users to create accurate, detailed, and precise designs and drawings of physical objects. It offers a wide range of tools for drafting, designing, and editing, including 2D and 3D modeling, drawing, and annotation features.

Autocad is widely used by architects, engineers, drafters, and other design professionals to create everything from simple floor plans and elevations to complex mechanical systems and construction projects.

An advanced Autocad training course teaches individuals how to use Autocad software to create, modify, and manipulate 2D and 3D designs. The training typically includes both theoretical and practical components, covering topics such as:

  • Introduction to Autocad software and interface
  • Drawing and drafting basics
  • 2D and 3D modeling
  • Annotation and dimensioning
  • Printing and publishing
  • Customization and automation
  • Collaboration and teamwork
