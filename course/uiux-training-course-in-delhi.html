What to Expect from the Curriculum:

Introduction to UI/UX:

Detailed Course Contents:

  • UI (User Interface) refers to the visual elements and interactive components of a digital product that users interact with.
  • UX (User Experience) focuses on the overall experience and satisfaction of users while interacting with a product or system.
  • Understanding the difference between UI and UX is crucial to grasp the holistic nature of UI/UX design.
User Research

Detailed course contents:

  • User research involves gathering insights about the target audience to inform design decisions.
  • User interviews and surveys help understand user needs, preferences, and pain points.
  • Creating user personas and scenarios helps visualize and empathize with target users.
  • Analyzing user behavior through data collection and observation guides the design process.
Information Architecture:

Detailed course contents:

  • Information architecture is about organizing and structuring content to enhance navigation and findability.
  • Sitemaps provide a visual representation of a website’s structure and hierarchy.
  • User flows depict the paths users take to achieve their goals within a system.
  • Wireframing is the process of creating low-fidelity layouts to outline the structure and basic functionality of a design.
Interaction Design

Detailed course contents:

  • Interaction design focuses on designing interactive elements that facilitate smooth user interactions.
  • Understanding Gestalt principles and visual perception helps create visually appealing and intuitive interfaces.
  • Micro-interactions are small, subtle design elements that provide feedback and enhance the user experience.
  • Transitions and animations contribute to the overall usability and engagement of a design
Visual Design

Detailed course contents

  • Visual design deals with the aesthetics and visual aspects of a product.
  • Color theory and color psychology help select appropriate color schemes that evoke desired emotions and enhance usability.
  • Typography involves choosing fonts and arranging text in a visually appealing and readable manner.
  • Layout and composition principles guide the arrangement of visual elements for balance and hierarchy.
Usability Testing and Evaluation

Detailed course contents:

  • Usability testing involves observing and analyzing user interactions with a design to assess its effectiveness.
  • Heuristic evaluation is a systematic inspection of a design’s usability based on established principles.
  • Usability tests help identify pain points, gather feedback, and validate design decisions.
  • A/B testing compares two or more design variations to determine which performs better
Mobile and Responsive Design

Detailed course contents:

  • Mobile design focuses on creating interfaces optimized for smaller screens and touch interactions.
  • Responsive design ensures that a design adapts to different screen sizes and devices.
  • Mobile-specific considerations include prioritizing content, designing touch-friendly interactions, and optimizing performance.
Accessibility

Detailed course contents:

  • Accessibility aims to make digital products usable by people with disabilities.
  • Understanding WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) helps design inclusive interfaces.
  • Inclusive design principles focus on creating products that are accessible to all users, regardless of disabilities or limitations.
UI/UX Tools

Detailed course contents:

  • Design software like Sketch, Figma, or Adobe XD are commonly used for creating UI/UX designs.
  • Prototyping tools like InVision, Marvel, or Framer facilitate interactive and realistic prototypes.
  • Collaboration and version control tools (e.g., Zeplin, Abstract) help streamline the design process within teams.
Industry Trends and Case Studies

Detailed course contents:

  • Staying updated with current design trends helps create modern and relevant designs.
  • Analyzing successful UI/UX case studies provides insights into effective design strategies and methodologies.
  • Design blogs, forums, and conferences offer opportunities to learn from industry experts and stay connected with the UI/UX community.

Trends in UI/UX Training

The field of UI/UX design is evolving rapidly, with training programs adapting to keep up with these changes. Here are some key trends shaping the future of UI/UX education: ...

Focus on User-Centered Design

Modern training programs emphasize creating designs that prioritize user experience. This includes understanding user behavior, psychology, and usability testing to ensure that designs are intuitive and effective.

Key Trends in UI/UX Training Programs:

  • Integration of Design Thinking and Agile Methodologies:UI/UX courses now incorporate design thinking and Agile methodologies to help students approach design challenges in a structured yet flexible way. These methodologies prepare designers for fast-paced environments where user feedback is constantly integrated into the design process.
  • Prototyping and Wireframing Emphasis:With tools like Figma, Sketch, and Adobe XD becoming industry standards, there is a strong focus on prototyping and wireframing. Students learn to create interactive prototypes that effectively communicate their designs to stakeholders and developers.
  • Mobile-First Design:As mobile usage continues to rise, training programs emphasize mobile-first design principles. Students are taught how to design responsive and adaptive interfaces that perform seamlessly across different devices.
  • AI and Automation in Design:With the growing role of AI-driven tools and automation in design, training programs are integrating these technologies into their curricula. Students learn how to leverage AI to optimize user experiences, automate repetitive tasks, and enhance personalization.
  • Real-World Projects and Portfolio Development:Hands-on learning through live projects and case studies is now a core element of UI/UX training. Students are encouraged to build strong portfolios that showcase their practical design work, which is vital for securing jobs in the competitive design industry.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Knowledge:UI/UX design is increasingly being integrated with other fields such as front-end development, branding, and marketing. Training programs are offering cross-disciplinary learning to ensure students understand how their design work fits into the broader digital ecosystem.
  • Certification and Placement Assistance:Many UI/UX programs now offer certifications that are recognized in the industry, paired with placement assistance to help students smoothly transition into the job market.

These trends underscore the increasing importance of user-centered design, real-world application, and innovation in the UI/UX field. At Uncodemy in Delhi, we ensure our curriculum is aligned with these trends, equipping students with the skills and experience they need to succeed in this dynamic industry.

Salary Packages for UI/UX Professionals

Salaries for UI/UX professionals vary based on factors such as experience, location, industry, and specialized skills. Below is an overview of the typical salary ranges for different roles in the UI/UX field: ...

UI/UX Designer Salary Breakdown:

  • Entry-Level UI/UX Designer:Fresh graduates or those with 1-2 years of experience typically earn between INR 3-6 lakhs per annum, depending on the company and location. These professionals typically work under the guidance of senior designers and contribute to the design process.
  • Mid-Level UI/UX Designer:With 3-5 years of experience, salaries range from INR 6-10 lakhs per annum. At this level, designers handle more complex projects, create user interfaces, conduct user research, and may take on leadership roles in design teams.
  • Senior UI/UX Designer:For professionals with over 5 years of experience, salaries typically range from INR 10-15 lakhs per annum. Senior designers are responsible for leading design projects, mentoring junior designers, and developing user-centered design strategies.
  • UX Researcher:Professionals specializing in user research and usability testing can earn between INR 6-12 lakhs per annum, depending on their expertise and industry. UX researchers play a crucial role in understanding user behavior and informing design decisions.
  • UI/UX Design Lead:These professionals manage design teams and ensure design projects align with the company’s vision and user needs. Salaries range from INR 12-18 lakhs per annum, based on the size and complexity of the projects they lead.
  • Product Designer:Product designers, who manage the complete design process from research to final product, typically earn between INR 10-18 lakhs per annum. Their role combines both UX and UI design and requires an in-depth understanding of user needs and business goals.
  • UX/UI Consultant:Freelancers or consultants providing expert advice on UI/UX strategies typically earn between INR 8-15 lakhs per annum, depending on their client base and expertise in specialized areas such as wireframing, design systems, or prototyping.
  • Mobile UI/UX Designer:Specializing in designing user interfaces for mobile applications, mobile UI/UX designers can earn between INR 8-16 lakhs per annum, based on experience and platform expertise.
  • Interaction Designer:Interaction designers, who focus on the user experience and interaction patterns of digital products, typically earn between INR 7-14 lakhs per annum, depending on their experience and skill set.
  • UI/UX Developer:Developers with UI/UX design skills typically earn between INR 7-14 lakhs per annum, depending on their expertise with front-end development frameworks and design tools.

Salaries for UI/UX professionals can grow significantly with experience, specialization, and expertise in areas like mobile design, interaction design, or advanced prototyping. Professionals working in leading companies or with high-level specialization can command even higher salaries.
