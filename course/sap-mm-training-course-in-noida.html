Set the Basics Right

The SAP MM (Materials Management) course curriculum in Noida typically covers a range of topics to equip students with comprehensive skills and knowledge needed for SAP MM implementation, configuration, and management. Here's a typical curriculum outline:

1. Introduction to SAP and ERP
  • - Overview of SAP ERP systems
  • - Introduction to SAP MM module and its role in business processes
  • - Basics of procurement, inventory management, and material valuation
2. SAP MM Organizational Structure
  • - Configuration of organizational units: Client, Company Code, Plant, Storage Location, Purchasing Organization, Purchasing Group
3. Master Data in SAP MM
  • - Material Master: Creation, maintenance, and views
  • - Vendor Master: Creation, maintenance, and types of vendors
  • - Info Record: Creation, maintenance, and use in purchasing
4. Procurement Process
  • - Purchase Requisition (PR): Creation, release strategies
  • - Request for Quotation (RFQ): Creation, distribution, and evaluation
  • - Purchase Order (PO): Creation, release strategies, types of POs
  • - Outline Agreements: Contracts and Scheduling Agreements
5. SAP Inventory Management
  • - Goods Receipt: Processes, inbound delivery, goods receipt posting
  • - Goods Issue: Processes, reservations, goods issue posting
  • - Physical Inventory: Types of physical inventory, cycle counting
6. Invoice Verification
  • - Invoice Processing: Verification against PO, goods receipt, and contract
  • - Credit Memos and Subsequent Debits/Credits
  • - Automatic Settlements (Evaluated Receipt Settlement - ERS)
7. Integration with Other SAP Modules
  • - Integration with SAP SD (Sales and Distribution) for order processing
  • - Integration with SAP PP (Production Planning) for material requirements planning
  • - Integration with SAP FI (Financial Accounting) for accounting entries
8. Reporting and Analytics in SAP MM
  • - Standard reports in SAP MM
  • - Customizing reports using SAP Query and SAP Analytics
9. Configuration and Customization
  • - Customization of SAP MM: Document types, number ranges, field selection
  • - Configuration of release procedures for PRs, POs, and invoices
  • - Special procurement processes: Subcontracting, consignment, pipeline
10. Case Studies and Project Work
  • - Practical exercises, case studies, and simulations to apply SAP MM concepts
  • - Real-world scenarios and project implementation in SAP MM
11. SAP MM Certification Preparation
  • - Preparation tips and guidance for SAP MM certification exams
  • - Practice tests and mock exams to assess readiness
12. Emerging Trends and Advanced Topics
  • - Introduction to advanced topics like SAP S/4HANA and SAP Ariba
  • - Emerging trends in SAP MM, such as cloud integration and analytics
