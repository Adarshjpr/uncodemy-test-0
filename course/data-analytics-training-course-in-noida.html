Why Data Analytics Course? What Will You Get?

Data Analytics is a process of transforming raw data into actionable insights using various technologies, tools, and processes. These are essential for identifying trends and solving complex problems by leveraging data accurately. As a method, Data Analytics helps enhance business decision-making for both established companies and startups. The process involves the following steps:

  • Determine your goal.
  • Understand the context around business and data.
  • Acquire an overview of the data.
  • Formulate specific hypotheses.
  • Test the hypotheses.
  • Validate results.

Data Analytics is divided into four key types:

  • Prescriptive
