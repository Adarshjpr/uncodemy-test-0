Set the Basics Right

Uncodemy’s Japanese language courses in Bangalore offer a well-rounded curriculum designed to cater to different proficiency levels, from beginners to advanced learners. The curriculum emphasizes practical skills, cultural understanding, and a deep dive into grammar, vocabulary, and conversational Japanese.

Topics and Units Covered

Our curriculum is divided into units and levels based on CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) standards:

Beginner Level (N5-N4)
  • Introduction to Hiragana and Katakana: Learn the two basic Japanese alphabets and their usage in writing and reading.
  • Basic Vocabulary and Phrases: Common greetings, introductions, and essential everyday phrases.
  • Fundamentals of Japanese Grammar: Sentence structure, basic verb forms, particles, and simple tenses.
  • Pronunciation and Listening Skills: Practice listening comprehension through simple conversations and dialogues.
  • Introduction to Kanji: Learn basic Kanji characters and their meanings.
Intermediate Level (N3)
  • Expanded Vocabulary: Learn more advanced vocabulary related to travel, work, and daily life.
  • Intermediate Grammar: Dive into more complex sentence structures, conjunctions, and expressions for varied situations.
  • Reading Practice: Short stories, news articles, and dialogues to enhance reading skills.
  • Speaking and Listening: Role-playing activities, group discussions, and listening exercises to improve fluency.
  • Cultural Insights: Explore Japanese customs, traditions, and etiquette in professional and social settings.
Advanced Level (N2-N1)
  • Advanced Kanji: Master hundreds of complex Kanji characters and understand their usage in context.
  • Complex Grammar and Expression: Learn formal and informal speech, advanced sentence structures, and keigo (respectful language).
  • In-depth Reading and Writing: Analyze longer texts, newspapers, and literature to hone reading comprehension.
  • Advanced Speaking and Listening: Engage in detailed conversations, debates, and presentations to enhance fluency.
  • Cultural and Business Etiquette: Study advanced cultural aspects, including professional Japanese communication and business practices.

Throughout each level, students will engage in interactive activities such as quizzes, assignments, and mock exams to reinforce their learning and prepare for the JLPT (Japanese-Language Proficiency Test).

Trends in Japanese language Training

The demand for Japanese language training has been growing globally, driven by factors like economic globalization, the rise of Japanese pop culture, and increased business opportunities between Japan and other countries. Here are some of the key trends in Japanese language ... training:
  • 1. Integration of Technology in Learning
    • Online and Hybrid Learning: The pandemic accelerated the shift to online learning, and many language institutes now offer hybrid programs (a mix of online and offline classes). This gives learners flexibility and access to native-speaking instructors from anywhere in the world.
    • Language Apps and AI Tools: Tools like Duolingo, Memrise, and Anki for flashcards have become popular for self-study. AI-powered platforms also offer personalized learning experiences, where the software adapts to the learner’s pace and difficulty level, making language acquisition more efficient.
  • 2. Increased Focus on Business and Technical Japanese
    • Business Japanese: With the growing number of Japanese businesses operating globally and expanding into markets like India, there’s a greater emphasis on business Japanese. Courses are focusing on polite forms of language (Keigo), etiquette, and expressions commonly used in the corporate world.
    • Industry-Specific Japanese: There's an increasing demand for technical Japanese in fields like IT, engineering, automotive, and medical sciences. This includes learning industry-specific terminology, communication styles, and technical jargon, preparing learners for specialized roles in multinational companies.
  • 3. Japanese Pop Culture Influence
    • Anime and Manga: The global rise in popularity of anime and manga is influencing language learning trends. Many learners are motivated to study Japanese to understand anime, manga, and video games in their original language, leading to a surge in younger students taking up the language.
    • J-Pop and Kawaii Culture: The appeal of J-Pop music, Japanese fashion, and Kawaii culture (cute culture) has made Japan more attractive as a cultural destination, thus inspiring people to learn the language to understand cultural nuances and engage with entertainment content.
  • 4. Certification and Professional Development
    • JLPT (Japanese Language Proficiency Test): The JLPT remains the most recognized standard for measuring Japanese proficiency worldwide. More language learners are preparing for the JLPT exams, which is also an important factor for job seekers aiming to work with Japanese companies.
    • Language Certifications for Career Growth: There is an increasing trend of corporate-sponsored language learning, where companies help employees learn Japanese to work with Japanese clients or partners. Language certifications are seen as valuable qualifications for career growth in sectors like technology, automotive, tourism, and international relations.
  • 5. Immersive Learning and Cultural Integration
    • Study Abroad Programs: There's a growing interest in language immersion programs in Japan, where students go abroad to live and learn the language directly in a native environment. This approach accelerates fluency and helps students understand Japanese culture deeply.
    • Cultural Sensitivity and Etiquette: Alongside language training, there is a focus on learning about Japanese culture, business etiquette, and communication practices. This helps learners not only speak the language but also interact effectively with Japanese speakers in different cultural settings.
  • Conclusion: The trends in Japanese language training reflect both globalization and cultural influence, as well as the growing need for business communication and specialized skills. With the increasing availability of learning tools and platforms, language learners now have more opportunities than ever to master Japanese in ways that fit their goals, whether personal or professional. As businesses and educational institutions continue to emphasize the importance of multilingualism, Japanese language training will likely remain a valuable skill in the global market.
