What Is SAP SCM Training, Why You Should Do It From Uncodemy?

SAP SCM (Supply Chain Management) training refers to education and skill development focused on SAP's suite of applications designed to manage and optimize supply chain processes within organizations. SAP SCM encompasses various modules and functionalities that help businesses streamline their supply chain operations, enhance efficiency, and improve collaboration with suppliers and customers.

Key components typically covered in SAP SCM training include:

1. Supply Network Collaboration (SNC) Facilitates collaboration with suppliers and partners to optimize supply chain processes such as procurement, inventory management, and demand planning.

2. Advanced Planning and Optimization (APO) Supports demand planning, supply network planning, production planning, and scheduling to align supply with demand effectively.

3. Global Available-to-Promise (GATP) Provides real-time information on product availability across the supply chain to promise accurate delivery dates to customers.

4. Event Management (EM)- Monitors and manages supply chain events in real-time to mitigate risks and ensure smooth operations.

5. Warehouse Management (WM)- Manages warehouse operations, including goods receipt, storage, and order fulfillment, integrated with other SAP modules.

6. Transportation Management (TM)- Optimizes transportation planning and execution, including route determination, freight cost calculation, and shipment tracking.

7. Inventory Management (IM)- Tracks and manages inventory levels across the supply chain to optimize stock levels and reduce holding costs.

SAP SCM training equips professionals with the knowledge and skills to implement, configure, and utilize these modules effectively. It is beneficial for supply chain managers, consultants, analysts, and IT professionals involved in supply chain optimization and management within organizations using SAP ERP systems.

Professionals are trained to manage change effectively and integrate SAP SCM solutions with existing ERP systems and business processes.
