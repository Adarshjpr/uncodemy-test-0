SEO, or search engine optimization, is the process of improving the ranking of a website on search engines like Google. The goal is to increase the visibility of the website and drive more traffic to it. This is done through a variety of techniques, including optimizing the website for keywords, building links, and creating high-quality content. SEO is an important part of SEO, as it helps businesses get found by potential customers online.

So, now that we have the basics covered, we can go into more detail on the various aspects of SEO. Starting with keywords, these are the words and phrases that people use to search for information on the internet. SEO is all about optimizing for the right keywords so that the website appears at the top of the search results when people search for those terms. This is done by researching and selecting the most relevant and popular keywords, then using them throughout the website's content and meta tags.
