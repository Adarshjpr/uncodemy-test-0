What Is Workday, Why You Should Do It From Uncodemy?

Workday is a structured program designed to educate individuals and organizations on how to effectively use Workday, a cloud-based enterprise management software. Workday offers solutions for human capital management (HCM), financial management, payroll, and other business functions. The training helps users understand how to navigate, configure, and optimize Workday to meet their specific business needs.

Key Components of Workday Training

1. Introduction to Workday

  • Overview of the platform, its features, and benefits.
  • Basic navigation and understanding of the user interface

2. Human Capital Management (HCM) Training

  • Managing employee data: hiring, onboarding, and maintaining employee records.
  • Compensation and benefits management.
  • Absence and time tracking.
  • Performance management and talent management.

3. Financial Management Training

  • Financial accounting and reporting.
  • Expense management.
  • Procurement and supplier management.
  • Financial planning and analysis.

4. Payroll Training

  • Configuring and managing payroll processes.
  • Compliance and tax management.
  • Payroll reporting and analytics.

5. Advanced Topics

  • Workday integrations: connecting Workday with other systems.
  • Security and compliance: role-based access control, data privacy.
  • Custom reports and analytics.
  • Workday Studio for custom development.

6. Practical Exercises and Real-World Scenarios

  • Hands-on practice with the Workday system.
  • Case studies and real-world examples to apply learning.

7. Certification Preparation

  • Preparing for Workday certification exams.
  • Review of key concepts and practice tests.
