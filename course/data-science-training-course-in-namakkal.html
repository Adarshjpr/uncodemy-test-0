Curriculum For Data Science Training Course

A well-structured Data Science training course curriculum in Namakkal typically encompasses the fundamental tools , techniques , and concepts required for analyzing and interpreting data . The course can be categorized into the following key modules :...
  • Introduction to Data Science : An overview of data science , its practical applications , and the responsibilities of a data scientist .
  • Mathematics and Statistics : Foundational principles of linear algebra , probability , descriptive statistics , and inferential statistics , which serve as the bedrock for data analysis .
  • Programming Languages : Instruction in widely used data science languages such as Python and R , covering essential libraries like NumPy , pandas , and scikit-learn .
  • Data Wrangling : Techniques for cleaning , transforming , and organizing data , addressing missing values , and ensuring data quality for analytical purposes .
  • Data Visualization : Tools such as Matplotlib , Seaborn , and Tableau to generate graphs , charts , and dashboards for visual interpretation of data insights .
  • Machine Learning : Both supervised and unsupervised learning paradigms , including algorithms like regression , classification , clustering , decision trees , random forests , and support vector machines .
  • Deep Learning : An introduction to neural networks , encompassing advanced topics such as convolutional neural networks ( CNNs ) and recurrent neural networks ( RNNs ) for processing complex data types like images and text .
  • Big Data Technologies : Working with extensive datasets using tools like Hadoop , Spark , and cloud platforms such as AWS and Azure .
  • Natural Language Processing ( NLP ) : Techniques to analyze and interpret textual data , including sentiment analysis , topic modeling , and text classification .
  • SQL and Database Management : Fundamental SQL for querying and managing relational databases , alongside NoSQL databases like MongoDB for handling unstructured data .
  • Capstone Projects : Real-world projects designed to apply the knowledge acquired throughout the course , typically involving comprehensive problem-solving using data science techniques .
  • Ethics and Data Privacy : Discussions on ethical considerations in data science , responsible AI practices , and data privacy regulations like GDPR .
