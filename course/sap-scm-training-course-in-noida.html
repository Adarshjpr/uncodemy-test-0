Here’s a structured outline of the curriculum:

1. Introduction to SAP ERP and SCM
  • Overview of SAP ERP system
  • Introduction to SAP SCM module
  • Role of SAP SCM in supply chain management
2. Organizational Structure and Master Data
  • SAP SCM organizational units (Supply Chain Planning, Supply Chain Execution)
  • Master data in SAP SCM: Material master, Vendor master, Customer master
3. Supply Network Planning (SNP)
  • Demand planning and forecasting techniques
  • Supply planning and network optimization
  • Integration with SAP APO (Advanced Planning and Optimization)
4. Production Planning and Detailed Scheduling (PP/DS)
  • Production planning strategies
  • Capacity planning and optimization
  • Shop floor scheduling and execution
5. Global Available-to-Promise (GATP)
  • ATP (Available-to-Promise) basics and configuration
  • Integration with sales order processing
  • ATP in distributed and centralized environments
6. Inventory Management and Optimization
  • Inventory planning and optimization techniques
  • Safety stock planning and management
  • Material requirements planning (MRP) in SAP SCM
7. Supplier Relationship Management (SRM)
  • Supplier collaboration and vendor management
  • Contract management and procurement processes
  • Integration with SAP MM (Materials Management)
8. Warehouse Management (WM)
  • Warehouse structure and organization
  • Goods receipt and issue processes
  • Warehouse optimization and inventory control
9. Transportation Management (TM)
  • Freight planning and optimization
  • Transportation execution and tracking
  • Carrier selection and freight cost management
10. SAP SCM Analytics and Reporting
  • Key performance indicators (KPIs) in supply chain management
  • Reporting tools and analytics for SCM
  • Real-time data integration and visualization
11. Integration with Other SAP Modules
  • Integration with SAP FI (Financial Accounting)
  • Integration with SAP SD (Sales and Distribution)
  • Inter-module data flow and transaction processing
12. Case Studies and Project Work
  • Practical exercises and simulations
  • Real-world scenarios and implementation challenges
  • Project-based learning to apply SCM concepts in business contexts
13. Certification Preparation
  • Exam preparation tips and techniques
  • Mock tests and practice exams
  • Guidance on achieving SAP SCM certification
