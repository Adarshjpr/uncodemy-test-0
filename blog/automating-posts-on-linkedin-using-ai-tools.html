Automating posts on LinkedIn using AI tools

Mr. Bambam Kumar Yadav 2 days ago
  • 14 comments
  • 11 min read

AI tools can help you create engaging posts, but they'll also automate the scheduling, optimizations, and performance tracking too. Plan and execute the fast-paced strategy you need to stay consistent, regularly engage, and strengthen your presence on LinkedIn without occupying hours of your day to either create or publish your content. 

In the following article, we are going to walk you through automating posts on LinkedIn using AI automation tools, review some of the best available tools, and provide some examples with strategies to achieve the best results possible.

Why Use Automation for LinkedIn Posts?

Before discussing the precise tools and techniques, it's imperative to outline why automation is essential to managing your content on LinkedIn.

Consistency is King

The LinkedIn algorithm regularly favours users who maintain consistent posting activity. By scheduling your posts in advance, you can continue to perform consistent posts even while travelling.

Time Management

You will now have the ability to create a week's or a month's worth of content in one sitting instead of manually posting a single piece of content every single day. By then posting this content for you, I tailored automation tools will save you lots of time!

Increase Engagement

Artificial intelligence apps will assist you in and figuring out when the best times are to post for maximum reach & engagement.

Diverse Content

You can generate ideas for all sorts of content (text posts, carousels, articles, videos, etc.) without worrying about getting "stuck" creatively, thanks to AI help.

Analytics Based Data Insights

Many automation platforms also provide backend analytics provided to show you what is working, so you can refine your output data-led strategy accordingly.

Categories of AI Tools for LinkedIn Automation

AI tools for LinkedIn typically fall into three types:

AI Content Generation Tools

  • These are tools to help you brainstorm, write posts, or recycle content.
  • Examples: ChatGPT, Jasper AI, Copy.ai

AI Scheduling and Automation Tools

  • These are platforms to schedule ahead of time, manage multiple accounts, and automate the resharing of high-performing content.
  • Examples: Buffer, Hootsuite, SocialBee, Publer

AI Analytics and Optimization Tools

  • They provide analytics around engagement, identify the ideal posting time, and give you information to improve your strategy and posting.
  • Examples: Shield Analytics, Taplio, Inlytics

Typically, businesses and creators will use a mixture of the three different types of tools to complete the entire section of content ideation → creation → scheduling → optimization.

Step-by-Step Guide: Automating LinkedIn Posts with AI

Now, let's step through a tangible process to automate your LinkedIn posting strategy with AI.

Step 1: Define Your Content Strategy.

Automation is most effective when it is aligned against a defined plan. Indicate your goals (brand awareness, lead generation, authority building, job hunting, etc.), your target audience (who are you speaking to? Recruiters? Clients? Peers?), your content themes (career tips, case studies, trends in the industry, your own story, etc.), and your posting frequency (3–5 times per week would be ideal for maximum reach).

Step 2: Generate Content Ideas with AI.

Use AI writing tools such as ChatGPT, Jasper, or Writesonic to generate a host of ideas for your content topics. For example:

  • Ask ChatGPT: “Give me 10 LinkedIn post ideas for a software engineer that wants to demonstrate their expertise in AI and cloud computing.”
  • Utilize Jasper's templates to outline professional posts and create engagement hooks.

Consider re-purposing existing content (blogs, tweets, scripts from YouTube, etc.) to convert the content into LinkedIn posts with an AI summarizer.

Step 3: Use AI to Write and Refine Your LinkedIn Posts.

AI can create LinkedIn posts in many different tones, such as formal, conversational, and story-telling. For instance:

  • Input: "Write a LinkedIn post about the importance of soft skills in engineering careers."
  • Final Output: A structured post incorporating a hook, insight, and call to action.

Be sure to add your own experiences, stories, and voice to any AI-generated outputs to make it personal. AI is a good starting point, but authenticity comes from you.

Step 4: Create Visuals with AI Tools

Visuals increase engagement on LinkedIn. AI-based design tools make it easy to create carousels, graphics, and banners. Tools like Canva (with Magic Design) or Beautiful.ai will happily assist with this! Tools like Pictory (converting text to short videos) or Synthesia (AI/AV images to video) provide visuals and easily create a variety of content for your LinkedIn presence.

Step 5: Schedule your Posts Automatically

Once you have your content created, now to set-up your posts for auto-scheduling to post with tools like Buffer, Hootsuite, SocialPilot, Publer, and more. These tools will allow you to:

  • Bulk upload content.
  • Select the optimal times to schedule your posts (some offer AI assistance with this).
  • Post content on different personal or company page LinkedIn accounts. 
  • With evergreen content reposting process automation capabilities. 

For instance, Buffer's AI assistant can help you find the best time to schedule each post based on the engagements of your previous posts.

Step 6: Track and optimize

The last step is to review and analyse the analytics of all your posts, either using LinkedIn analytics, or with a third-party choice like Shield, or Inlytics.AI will help you to assess several factors including:

  • What type of post format works best for you (text, carousel, video, etc.)?
  • What topics get the most engagement?
  • What are the best times to post?
  • What are your audience growth trends?

Then you can tweak your strategy to gain an even greater outcome!

Best AI Tools for Automation on LinkedIn

Here’s a compiled list of the best AI tools to automate LinkedIn, organized by function:

1. Content Creation

  • ChatGPT - to generate post ideas, drafts, and engagement hooks
  • Jasper AI - provides templates specifically optimized for content on LinkedIn
  • Copy.ai - to obtain ideas quickly with multiple tone options available for posts

2. Scheduling & Publishing

  • Buffer - is an easy, beginner's way of utilizing a scheduling platform, options for AI suggested posts. 
  • Hootsuite - essentially any potential large and small brand will seek insight and use for teams and agencies.
  • SocialBee - posts by category with AI generated utilization of the scheduling tool.
  • Publer - is a reasonably priced scheduling tool you can automate through bulk scheduling.  

3. Analytics & Insights

  • Shield Analytics - offers the most advanced insights into LinkedIn personal profiles, etc
  • Inlytics - provides engagement heat maps, along with progress tracking for growth 
  • Taplio- takes standard letter and post-matching but combined with other analytics and AI lead generation. 

4. Visual Content Creation

  • Canva AI- uses templates, carousels, and proximal banners with AI suggestions for design concepts you can then use to create LinkedIn content
  • Pictory - to provide video as a result of text script automatically by AI 
  • Synthesia - use AI to create video presenters, large selected presenters, in now a studio standard or common in LinkedIn professional video.

Example: Automation of one week of LinkedIn posts

Suppose you are a marketing professional

 Here's how you can automate one week of content on LinkedIn:

  • Monday: Ask ChatGPT for a motivational post about leadership and schedule it with Buffer.
  • Tuesday: Use Canva AI to turn one of your blog posts into a LinkedIn carousel and schedule with Publer.
  • Wednesday: Publish an industry insight generated through Jasper and schedule on Hootsuite.
  • Thursday: Publish a short video created with Pictory and automate the upload to LinkedIn through SocialPilot.
  • Friday: Post a storytelling post about a personal career lesson you cleaned up with ChatGPT.
  • On Sunday you can check your analytics through Shield to see which one of your posts resonated the most with your audience.

Effective Methods for LinkedIn Automation with AI

Don’t 100% Rely on AI

  • Always add your personal touch—whether it be a story, opinion or experience. AI is a helper, not a replacement.

Interacting With Users

  • Automation takes care of the posting, but you should still engage with your audience personally through responding to comments and sending out personal messages.

Stay Within the Guidelines of LinkedIn

  • Automation is fine, but you can risk being ineffective if your automation is spammy (i.e. automated connection requests), this may breach LinkedIn's regulations.

Diversity in Content

  • Different types of content keep users engaged. Alternate between text, carousels, polls, and videos.

Test and Learn

  • Use analytics to learn and develop your strategy. What works for one audience might not work for another.

With the advancement of AI

The outlook for LinkedIn automation is looking even clearer. In the coming months, we will likely witness:

  • AI-enhanced personalization, such as posts specifically built for audience segments.
  • Automated comment replies either through AI chat bots or some other manner based on context.
  • Analytics transitioning into predictive analytics so instead of only recommendations for posting times/days we will have predictive engagement notifications.

AI is a powerful tool, but at the end of the day, your authenticity will always be your differentiator. Audiences respond to actual human voices, not robotic messaging. The correct applications of AI will have you off-loading all of the repetitive functions and continue to explore your own creativity and connections.

Conclusion 

As a professional, business, or creator, having a consistent and competitive presence on LinkedIn means leveraging artificial intelligence in LinkedIn marketing is no longer a matter of luxury, but a necessity. When you combine AI content generators and AI platforms that can also help with content scheduling, and analytics tools to help you stay organized, you can work less and give yourself the best chance of maximizing the overall visibility and engagement you want on LinkedIn.

The key is finding balance. Use AI to do the heavy lifting for you, but make sure your viewpoints, experiences, and true identity all shine through in every post. If you achieve this balance, leveraging automation on LinkedIn will allow you to transform your profile into an auto-generator of a powerful personal brand.
