Top digital tools for student productivity

Mr. Bambam Kumar Yadav1 days ago
  • 13 comments
  • 10 min read

It is not uncommon to be stressed at university where Tasks and study are balanced against paid work, and social life so that it is possible to live so busy that we are not certain how we can manage to attend lectures and pass our work, go out with friends and have enough time and energy to be able to make it worth the bother after all. Productivity tools like digitized ones assure an organized system facing workload, which guarantees students to concentrate on what matters most. Incorporating these tools in everyday life can make one work more efficiently, manage time more effectively and gain some time to have fun and relax.In contemporary educational settings, digital tools play a critical role in eliminating some of the challenges facing students that include procrastination, distractions, and poor planning. Thanks to the research, it is often emphasized that such tools are essential to modern researching processes, career development, and subsequent more efficient communication, coordination, performance measuring, and reporting. Indeed, a survey done in 2019 revealed that productivity apps ranked top as the most widely used apps with 97 percent of the participants using the apps widely by students and workers. Through these tools, the students are not only able to improve their output; they also acquire acquaintances with some skills that are needed in the professional world.

Best Online Tools that make students productive

The number of digital tools is overwhelming as each of them features different features to accommodate different areas of life as a student.

The next groups glean some of the most priceless academic success tools:

Task organization and management

Successful management of tasks is central to making sure that one is on top of academic needs. These applications assist the students to make to-do lists, manage tasks, and establish deadlines.

Todoist:This app is useful as it can assist the student in making to-do lists, sorting tasks using projects or the course, and deadline setting. The natural language input will enable the user to type such phrases as, Finish research by Friday, and the app will automatically schedule it. The ability to mark priorities in Todoist ensures that students are better able to prioritize tasks and deadlines, and the flexibility it has with other tools such as Google Calendar can be used to plan the deadlines in conjunction with daily routines. The application can be downloaded at App Store and Google Play at no cost.

Microsoft To Do Designed to work on both a desktop and a phone, Microsoft To Do will allow the students to create detailed to-do lists, deadlines and it will allow prioritizing their tasks. Its task delegation and group work features facilitate group assignments at work and its daily planner manifestation of the product helps students to manage the day. It is free to use Microsoft To Do.

Trello:Trello is a project management tool in which one can organize tasks and projects using boards, lists, and cards. It is especially helpful when handling complex projects such as group assignments, making the students able to view all the tasks and deadlines at a glance. Students can make boards per each class or project and include lists of various steps, also moving cards when the tasks are accomplished, which makes them more accountable and collaborative. Trello has collaborations with such tools as Google Drive and Slack, making collaboration more secure. There is a free plan.

My Homework Student Planner:This application is a complete university student companion and it provides a calendar to take note of important appointments, exam schedules, and project deadlines. It has a Homework section where the assignments and projects can be recorded, and the students can encode the class schedules and grades so as to monitor progress. Being a digital planner, it notifies users about the oncoming classes and deadlines.

Information management and Note-Taking

Academic success cannot rule out the importance of having organized notes and therefore they present powerful tools to capture and manage information.

Notion:Notion is a one-stop work app that suits well to consolidate notes, projects and to-dos lists. It enables one to have single pages on subjects, folder notes, and joining up with classmates. The database system of Notion is able to follow the progress of the projects, to have reading lists, and composition revision checklists as all the information is synced on each of the devices so that the reader could access the information. Notion provides models of coursework monitoring and exam preparation. Its high levels of customization might be confusing at the start, but it can be molded to fit in different workflows.

Evernote:Evernote is a digital binder through which a student can keep coursework and notes safely in one place. It allows using voice memos, textbook scans, screenshots, and photos without leaving the notes. Users have the ability to clip PDFs and websites and notes are synchronized on all devices, and they can go offline. Evernote can also be used to create lists of to do, tagging of notes to be able to find them with ease and splitting of notes into digital notebooks. It has strong search capabilities that are able to search in hand written notes and pictures.

Microsoft OneNote:OneNote is another strong note-taking application that enables students to isolate notes based on the subject, highlight important details and include voice notes. It is accessible in almost all the devices, and thus the notes are always at hand. OneNote can be used along other Microsoft office tools such as Word and Excel and one can easily have a smooth flow in the work processes.

Google Keep:This simple note-making application fits into the Google world, and it enables users to make use of the notes, lists, and reminders, which are available at any device. It allows voice memos, the attachment of images as well as color coding to make it easier to keep. Google Keep is applicable as an instant note taking, team notes, and reminder based on the location.

Supernotes:Supernotes allows notes to be grouped in context of notecards which can be tagged, linked, and shared allowing the easy expansion of course notes and relation between different concepts at hand. It is quick and available on every device so that it is possible to collaborate with the classmates in real-time.

Tools of focus and concentration.

It is not easy to stay on track when there are digital distractions, yet, these devices allow one to reduce the amount of interruptions and focus better.

Freedom:Freedom disables distractive sites and applications on all devices. They can manually compile blocklists or set focus time so that distractions such as social media or unnecessary web surfing get filtered out.

Opal:Like Forest, Opal is a concentration app that makes focusing fun by rewarding users by growing a virtual gem like the ones in Minecraft when they stay productive. It provides blocking of apps/ websites in real-time to eliminate distraction.

Cooperation and Communication Tool

Team work is a sad experience at college, and group work tools make team work and communicating with others faster and easier.

Slack:The application is already widely spread in the business context, but is also useful on group projects and study groups. It enables the creation of channels, sharing files, and live communication, which combine with such tools as Google Drive and Trello.

Calendly:Calendly also connects to online calendars (Google Calendar, iCloud Calendar, Microsoft 365) to display availability, so recipients can, without back-and-forth dialogue, choose a meeting time that can fit everybody. Its rudimentary version is complimentary.

Aids to Writing and Research

To be an effective writer, research is an important requirement in the achievement of an academic goal. These are tools, which offer support in terms of grammar, readability, and source management.

Intellecs.ai:The tool based on AI helps students to create practical study guides and summaries by reading lecture notes and textbooks. It is time-saving as one can summarize text automatically and get core points to study to pass an exam.

Grammar Checker by DupliChecker:It is a free checking tool that works fast in identifying grammar, spelling, and punctuation mistakes then provides a real-time suggestion to clarify and improve the quality of writing.

Learning Aids

The digital world allows making the studying process more active and productive, providing a variety of options for retaining information.

Otter.ai / Fireflies.aiThese are AI-based tools that convert a lecture or meeting to notes and summaries that can be searched.They allow students to concentrate on listening instead of typing, as well as set up automatic to-do lists required with group projects. Otter is particularly designed to do real-time transcripts, as well as search them.

Canva:Although Canva can be used to create a design, it can also be applied in drawing graphs and tables in research work or a presentation to visually explain information. Canva for Education is both students and teachers free.
