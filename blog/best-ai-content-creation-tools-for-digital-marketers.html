Best AI Content Creation Tools for Digital Marketers

Mr. Bambam Kumar Yadav 2 days ago
  • 14 comments
  • 11 min read

In this article, we will go through the top AI content creation tools that every digital marketer should know about! There is an AI tool for everything from written text to image design, video editing, search engine optimization, and social media content; meaning digital marketers can work smart, not hard.

1. ChatGPT by OpenAI.

Overview:

ChatGPT is one of the most advanced conversational AI models I have seen to date. Marketers are now using ChatGPT for generating ideas, writing blog articles, working on ad copies, product descriptions, and even video scripts.

Key Features:

  • Natural language text generation.
  • Flexibility in taking tone, style, and context into account.
  • Ability to use plugins and APIs to extend use cases.
  • Great for blogs, social media, customer support, and email marketing.

A use case:

A marketer has to support an e-commerce business who is launching a new clothing line. Instead of typing in dozens of product descriptions by hand, the marketer can simply tell ChatGPT to generate multiple SEO-friendly product descriptions in a style and tone for each target audience in seconds.

2. Jasper AI

Summary

Jasper AI (formerly known as Jarvis), is a highly popular content-generating software platform for marketers and businesses and is very effective at producing long-form content like blog articles, eBooks, and newsletters.

Key Features

  • Has pre-built marketing templates (AIDA, PAS, blog outlines).
  • Can recognize and generate content in a variety of languages.
  • SurferSEO integration to generate keyword optimized content.
  • It is a collaborative workspace to allow teams to work together.

Use Case Example

A digital marketing agency could use Jasper to generate blog articles for clients or their own agency's website every week with the appropriate keywords set into the articles, alongside less time spent with quality output.

3. Copy.ai

Summary

Copy.ai only works with short-form content animal, like ad copies, headlines, email subjects and social media capture. Due to its simplicity, usability and creativity Copy.ai is super popular with startups and social media marketers.

Key Features

  • Templates for ads, emails, and products description.
  • Brainstorming features for slogans and taglines.
  • Easy for beginners.

Use Case Example

A social media manager who is running campaigns on Instagram can quickly generate 10 different variations of captions meant for different A/B testing, which will yield more engagement potential.

4. Writesonic

Overview

Writesonic is an AI Copywriting platform that can be used for short-form and long-form marketing copy. Writesonic is designed with SEO optimization and chat-based content creation included as well.

Key Features

  • Long-form article writer.
  • AI based landing page generator.
  • Image and ad copy creator.
  • Integrated ChatSonic - a ChatGPT-like assistant.

Use Case Consideration

For the digital marketer building a launch landing page for both benefits and issues associated with a product, Writesonic can generate headline versions, clear CTAs, and CARO keyword specific content in a matter of minutes. 

5. SurferSEO

Overview

Though not itself a text generator, SurferSEO uses AI to optimize written content for search engines like Google. SurferSEO's function is to assess competitors, identify keywords, and analyse SERP performance to generate "actionable insights" that can improve ranking.

Key Features

  • Real-time SEO content editing options.
  • Keyword density and semantic keyword suggestions.
  • SERP analysis that compares competitors.
  • Ability to integrate with Jasper and write in an SEO optimized way.

Use Case Example

If a marketing team is writing a blog about "AI in healthcare," they could utilize SurferSEO to identify key words and lay out the structure of their article so they can identify and use the most effective keywords causing them to rank higher on Google.

6. Grammarly

Overview

Grammarly is much more than a grammar checker, as they leverage AI to help increase readability, engagement, as well as tone. For marketers this helps keep content professional and polished.

Key Features

  • Grammar, spelling and punctuation errors checking.
  • Tone checking to ensure writing matches the audience.
  • Style and clarity improvement.
  • Plagiarism checking to ensure originality.

Use Case Example

A content writer writing LinkedIn posts could then take some already published content and simply change the tone from casual to formal to fit the intended brand voice on another platform with Grammarly.

7. Canva AI (Magic Studio)

Overview

Canva has built into their platform, a variety of new AI powered design tools to make it easier for marketers to create visuals without needing advanced design skills. Magic Studio allows the user to generate images from text, design suggestions, and video creation.

Key Features

  • Magic Design provides easy automated templates.
  • AI powered image editing/generation.
  • Automated social media graphics.
  • User-Friendly drag/drop interface.

Use Case Example

A digital marketer could easily summarize blog content into an infographic using Canva's AI features, thus creating a visually engaging piece for social channels.

8.Lumen5

Overview

Lumen5 is an artificial intelligence-based video production framework that creates videos from written content. Video marketing is increasingly important, and this application helps marketers create video content by repurposing blog and article content.

Key Features

  • AI-based text-to-video production.
  • Library of music, stock videos, and stock images.
  • Automated selection of scenes and captions.
  • Branding adjustments.

Use Case Sample

If a marketing team has a blog that is 1,000 words long, they can convert the content into a short explainer video for YouTube or LinkedIn to distribute the content beyond just the blog.

9.Pictory AI

Overview

Pictory is another robust AI video generator that can create standard clips from long videos or extract highlights for social media.

Key Features

  • Automatic summarization of videos.
  • Subtitle and caption generation.
  • Script to video transformation.
  • Ideal for repurposing webinars, podcasts.

Use Case Sample

If a marketer is managing the webinar, they would use Pictory to convert long-form content to short promo clips for Twitter and Instagram stories to develop the clip exposure.

10. MarketMuse

Overview MarketMuse uses AI to enhance your content marketing strategy. Unlike a normal keyword suggestion tool, MarketMuse offers topic modelling, competitive analysis, and content scoring.

Key Features

  • AI-driven content briefs.
  • Content optimization scores.
  • SERP and competitor analysis.
  • Strategy recommendations for content gaps.

Use Case Example:

 If a company is planning to build authority in digital marketing, MarketMuse will identify subtopics, such as AI in influencer marketing, which may not have a lot of curated content on it. This helps plan future content well.

11. Frase.io

Overview Frase.io combines AI-powered research plus SEO-oriented content optimization. It produces topic briefs and analyses competitors' content to allow you to rank better.

Key Features

  • AI-powered content briefs.
  • Investigation into questions for SEO FAQs.
  • AI text generator to create outlines and posts.
  • Suggestions for real-time optimization.

Use Case Example A freelance marketer is creating blog posts for a SaaS company. Frase.io helps the completion of briefs in minutes, saving them hours of manual content research.

12. Synthesia

Description

Synthesia allows marketers to create AI video content with human-like avatars to communicate their intended messages. It takes the hassle out of hiring actors, finding a studio, and planning the entire video shoot.

Key Features

  • AI-human avatars and voiceovers.
  • Support for many languages.
  • Custom branding.
  • Quick to produce videos.

Example of a use case

An international client can create localized video ads in multiple languages with Synthesia's avatars, and reach a very large global audience with consolidated production cost.

13. DALL·E 3 by OpenAI

Description

DALL·E 3 is the latest in AI systems that create images with high quality from simple text prompts. For digital marketers, this is a never-ending supply of advertisements, blog illustrations, and social media graphics.

Key Features

  • Text to image generator.
  • Image editing and refining.
  • High resolution and brand-specific images.
  • Easily integrates with ChatGPT.

Example of a use case

A content marketer uses DALL·E 3 to quickly create unique illustrations for a blog on "future technology" that enhances the originality of the content and avoids the use of stock photos.

14. HubSpot Content Assistant

Overview

HubSpot Content Assistant utilizes AI capabilities which live within HubSpot's platform allowing marketers to write blogs, emails, and social posts, directly in their preferred workflow.

Key Features

  • Write marketing copy created by AI.
  • Send personalized emails based on customer data.
  • Design tightly integrated with HubSpot CRM.
  • Optimize performance using analytics.

Use Case Example

A B2B marketer prepares outreach emails with AI that help trigger personalization which ultimately leads to higher engagement rates with no manual writing time.

15. QuillBot

Overview

QuillBot is an AI paraphrasing and summarizing tool that helps you get your content to the point of clarity and originality.

Key Features

  • AI paraphrasing modes (formal, creative, concise).
  • Summarize long documents.
  • Free grammar check and plagiarism detector.
  • Chrome extension for quick access to editing features.

Use Case Example

A content writer updating stale blog content would easily get full use of QuillBot to rewrite the paragraphs in a new style, keep it fresh for SEO to prevent duplicating content.

The Benefits of AI Content Tools for Digital Marketers

Efficiency - When you realize you can use AI content tools to remove the repetitive aspects of any job like copywriting, keyword research, video editing, etc., you can be more efficient with your time.

Cost Savings - As a result of cutting down on extensive teams of creatives, cost is minimized.

Scalability - AI content tools allow you to produce vast amounts of content from different sources without sacrificing quality so you have the potential to maximize scalability.

Personalization - With the help of AI content tools, you can personalize unique content for your intended audience.

Data-Driven Decisions - AI content tools, such as MarketMuse and SurferSEO, allow marketers to use data to make strategic content choices.

Challenges and Considerations

Although AI content Tools hold incredible potential for marketers, they must also be mindful of the following:

  • Originality: Marketers and brands can easily fall into an over-reliance on AI content tools, which results in baseline content that is not unique.
  • Accuracy: AI content generators create text that sometimes requires checking every single thing in the data in order to be accurate.
  • Brand Voice: In order to accurately represent your brand voice, and to ensure consistency across every platform, marketers will still require human moderation of content.
  • Why is AI Content Important: It is acceptable to be transparent in your content and disclose to your audience the purpose of AI generated content.

Conclusion

We can see how Artificial Intelligence has changed the face of digital marketing offering tools that save us time, money, and even increase creativity. When it comes to text-based generation including top apps ChatGPT, Jasper and Copy.ai. SEO optimization using tools SurferSEO, Frase and MarketMuse. Visual design; Canva, DALL·E and Synthesia. Video creation; Lumen5, Pictory and so on. All of these examples are AI-powered platforms that can fit the whole content creation workflow.

However, for digital marketers, use of AI shouldn't necessarily replace human creativity but to enhance human creativity using the capabilities that AI provides. Ben with these digital economy resources gives marketers more time to focus on innovation storytelling and authentic connection with the audiences. Clearly, AI will fuel the future of digital marketing. Marketers that adapt to using these tools now will be the leaders and innovators of tomorrow.
