What Is Tally?

So, Tally is one of the most popular accounting software which is widely used in many countries, including India. Use of Tally

This is one of the most powerful accounting software programs that easily provides solutions to various challenges of a business. Tally generally takes care of all the tasks that are crucial for enterprise management purposes. Some of the most usefull activities,, like bank reconciliation, accounts receivable and payable, and record keeping, are accessible and easily available in this software.

  1. What are the most important features of Tally software?

    Here are some of the most important features of Tally software :

    • For automation of employee records management using the payroll feature is a great choice anytime.
    • It is easy to update transactions automatically that are maintained at multiple locations.

    • Some of the additional features, like cheques and cash vouchers, are available.
    • Management of costs, looking for a profit centre for even better ledger management purposes.

    • Generation of tax ledger.
