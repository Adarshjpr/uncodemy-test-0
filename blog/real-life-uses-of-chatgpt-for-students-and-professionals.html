Real-Life Uses of ChatGPT for Students and Professionals

  • Mr. Irsad 3 days ago
  • 15
  • 11 min read

Real-life uses of students.

  • Study assistance and homework help Now, all those days are gone from flipping through multiple sets of textbooks or even skimming Google for hours. Students are just now asking ChatGPT for direct explanations of some interesting, complex concepts.

    • Example use: “Explain Newton's law in simple words with some interesting examples.” ChatGPT will give us instant answers that will save time and also improve understanding. It just breaks down difficult topics into some digestible parts. You can do this with other analogies and examples also.

  • Writing as well as editing an assignment Students often used this ChatGPT to :
    • Draft essays
    • Structure a research paper
    • Generate a thesis statement
    • Get both grammar and style suggestions

    • Tip - Instead of using ChatGPT to copy the whole set of answers, use it as a starting point, then be ready to add original insights to make your work authentic. Example - Can you help me write a 500-word essay on climate change and environmental sustainability with a reference?

  • Exam preparation and mock tests Do you require a quick quiz on thermodynamics? Do you want a practice set of questions for your GRE or SAT? ChatGPT will help you in simulating mock tests with explanations that will help students self-assess on a real-time basis.

    • Example - “Give me a set of 10 multiple-choice questions with answers for Python programming.” This is simply like having your virtual tutor 24*7 with no appointment required.

  • Learning new skills From learning different coding languages to improving their writing style or public speaking, students often use ChatGPT to do so that they can acquire new skills in a guided way.
    • Try asking - “Teach me HTML in 5 days just with practice exercises," “Give me tips to improve my fluency in spoken English.”
  • Resume and cover letter writing Almost 90% of all college students almost 90 % use ChatGPT for searching for their first internships or even jobs. This helps them in polishing their resume and drafting cover letters impressively.
    • Sample prompt for learning ease : Create a resume for a final year computer engineering student having experience in web development and also python projects.

Real-life uses for professionals.

This ChatGPT is not just helping students; it is also streamlining the lives of many professionals across industries. Whether you are a software Engineer, a marketer, an entrepreneur, or even a financial analyst, so use case is available waiting for you.

  • Email and communication drafting Do you require a tricky client email or a professional apology? Many professionals often use ChatGPT to draft
    • Formal letters
    • Follow-up messages
    • Formal. emails
    • Meeting invitation
    • LinkedIn connection notes

    • Prompt - Wrote a polite follow-up email to the recruiter after an interview.

    This will just help in saving time and also avoiding any kind of miscommunication. Highly useful when English is not your first language.

  • Report summaries and documentation

    • Professors are often required to read as well as interpret long reports, technical manuals, or even some legal documents. ChatGPT can just summarize and simplify all of them.in seconds.

    Prompt example :

    “Summarize this one of the 15-page reports into 5 bullet points.”

    ChatGPT can help us in drafting documents, preparing for some slide decks, and also auto-generate content from a set of meeting notes.

  • Data analysis and spreadsheet support

    • From.set of Excel formulas to understanding some of the queries professionals in finance, operations, and IT use ChatGPT for :

    • For writing a complex set of formulas
    • Creation of dashboards
    • Explaining some interesting data trends
    • Automating repetitive tasks

    Prompt example :

    “Write an Excel formula to just calculate CAGR from a huge list of values.”

  • Brainstorming and the best creative ideation

    • Content creators, marketers, and designers just love ChatGPT for their endless creativity.

    You can just use if for :

    • Generating various blog posts
    • Develop as well as copy
    • Don't forget to create some interesting and catchy headlines
    • Brainstorm some campaign ideas

    Prompt example:

    “Give me some of the 5 Instagram captions that are best suitable for a skincare product launch.”

    Bonus tip - You can easily instantly tweak the tone - bold, professional, quirky, or bold ones.

  • Language translation and localisation

    • Professionals with a global set of teams just use ChatGPT to translate documents, messages, and emails. This is also quite useful for understanding some cultural nuances in communication.

    Prompt example:

    “Translate this marketing email just from French to Spanish and then make it sound natural to other native speakers.”

    • Feedback on answers
    • STAR-based responses
    • Other role-specific queries

    Prompt :

    “Give me about 10 behavioral interview questions for a data analyst role, just with sample answers.”

Things to keep in mind

As we have seen, ChatGPT is quite incredible and helpful, but it is not perfect at all. Here are some more important tips that will help you think wisely.

Use ChatGPT for the below :

  • Summarize and revise
  • Kickstart tasks
  • Enhance productivity
  • Learn or brainstorm different ideas

Don't use ChatGPT for :

  • Replacing the critical set of thinking
  • Spreading some misinformation
  • Plagiarize work or assignments
  • Rely on some outdated facts ( always just double-check)
