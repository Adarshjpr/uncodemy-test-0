How to Use ChatGPT to Prepare for Job Interviews

  • Mr. Irshad Khan/ 3 days ago
  • 15
  • 11 min read

In this blog, we will particularly explore how you can use ChatGPT to prepare effectively for interviews, research companies, practice some common sets of questions, tailor resumes, and even build your confidence. You can do all this by staying in control of learning.

Why use ChatGPT for interview preparation work?

Just think of ChatGPT as your AI mentor who will always be available, be endlessly patient, and also have full up to date knowledge. Here are reasons why it is a game changer.

  • Custom mock interview
  • Role-specific preparation
  • Confidence-building exclusive feature
  • Resume and cover letter writing
  • Instant feedback is also given on answers.
  • You also get tips for behavioral and other technical rounds.

Let's break all this down step by step.

Step 1 - Start with a particular role-specific research

Before applying or interviewing for any of the roles, it is quite important to understand the job description and also some expectations from it.

You can just use ChatGPT to :

  • Explain some confusing types of jargon
  • Suggest some key important skills required for your role
  • Don't forget to summarise the job description in plain, simple English language
  • Lost some commonly asked set of interview questions for that particular position.

Example :

“Explain this part job description for a Data Analyst role in simple,, plain words.”

For the above thing prompt given to ChatGPT will give a response like below :

“Sure! This role is about collecting as well as analyzing huge data to help your team or you make informed decisions for better prospects. You have to be good at some trending tools like Excel, SQL, and Python in programming. Communication skills are quite important since you will be sharing all your findings with others.”

Now with ,this you will be more confused, but will he strategically inform too in the meantim?

Step 2 - Practice a common set of interview questions

Do you want to get better at some most likely asked questions like - “Tell me about yourself” or even “What are your greatest strengths as well as weaknesses?”

So, ChatGPT will help you in practising in a conversational style and give you some real-time solutions.

Don't forget to try this :

You will tell :

“Let’s do a mock HR interview now for a marketing associate job role. You will play the role of interviewer.”

For the above question, ChatGPT will answer as below :

“Sure! Let’s begin now.

Q1: Tell me about yourself.”

.

.

.

.

The way you respond then ChatGPT will provide you with constructive feedback accordingly. You also have the liberty to ask questions like

“Rate my answer and also suggest some improvements.”

All this will allow you to :

  • Avoid cliché types of phrases
  • Add both metrics or also examples
  • In time, your tone

Just over time, you will build a better muscle memory, best suitable for strong and concise responses.

Step 3 - Prepare yourself for technical interviews

If you are in IT, engineering, or even finance, or any other technical field, then technical rounds are quite intimidating.

Here is how ChatGPT will help you out :

  • Review your solutions in the best possible way
  • Generate some mock technical type of questions.
  • Explain interesting coding questions, or also some related case studies.
  • For situational problems, some sample answers will also be provided.

Example :

You: “Give me just 5 SQL sets of questions for a fresher Data Analyst role with solutions.”

ChatGPT: “Sure, and a list will be displayed here of your questions.

Retrieve the second-highest salary from the employee table and give sql command for that... Answer: SELECT MAX(salary) FROM employees WHERE salary < (SELECT MAX(salary) FROM employees);”

It will not only provide questions but also teach you how to approach a particular type of question.

Step 4 - Master behavioral interviews with the STAR method.

Companies often love behavioral questions nowadays to assess how you react in some real-time situations.

So in this, the STAR ( Situation, task, action, result ) is often their best friend in all such scenarios.

In this, ChatGPT can help you :

  • Understand the STAR framework.
  • Turn some vague type of memories into interesting, structured stories.
  • Based on your experience, you can create STAR responses.

An example of this is :

“Help me answer: Tell me about a time when you just handled a difficult customer, by using the STAR method.”

To the above question, ChatGPT answered as below :

“Sure!

Situation: While working as a summer sales intern, I encountered an angry client whose problem was that his order was delayed.

Task: I had to de-escalate this type of situation and ensure a timely resolution for this problem.

Action: I then apologized after that, offered a discount, coordinated with the logistics team, and kept the client updated about the same.

Result: The client appreciated this type of service and placed another set of two more orders the next month.”

So big boom! Now you have got concise and concrete answer to your problem.

Step 5 - Main step to personalize your resume and cover letter both

Customisation is key, and ChatGPT will help us in doing this by taking inputs from us and then designing things the way we want. We have noticed that generic applications often get ignored.SoS,o ChatGPT, will help you in tailoring your resume as well as your cover letter as per the job role and description to the particular job that you are applying for.

Some pro tips are as follows :

“Does this particular match the JD and role both for a risk analyst at an XYZ company?”

You can now ask for action verbs and also some quantifiable impact along with that.

Request a personalized type of cover letter based on job role + job description, + resume.

Suppose that you give below prompt :

“Write a cover letter for a person with an MBA finance graduate qualification applying to Morgan Stanley for an analyst job role.”

So ChatGPT will answer like this, as mentioned below :

“Sure!

Dear Hiring / Recruiting Manager,

I’m writing to express my sincere interest in the Analyst job role opening at Morgan Stanley…”

You can also modify tone, structure, as welll. as style based on the preferences.

Step 6 - Practice body language tips, and also some communication-related

Even some of the best answers may fall flat if communication skills are lacking a bit. Confident body language is also required.

As we know that ChatGPT can't see you at all, but still it will help you out :

  • Rephrasing long long-winded set of answers
  • Offer some public speaking tips
  • Some tech mirroring techniques are also available
  • Provide dos and don'ts for body language.

You suppose to give below prompt :

“How to sound best in a confident way in a Zoom-type interview?”

So ChatGPT will respond in below way :

Use some short and clear sentences for your needs. Smile naturally in a confident way. Maintain direct eye contact with the camera in front of you. Sit upright in an erect manner. Avoid any type of filler in keywords like ‘um’ or even ‘you know.’”

Step 7: Create a daily practice schedule

One of the best ways to use ChatGPT for preparation is to build consistency. Ask ChatGPT to provide and deliver you a personalized weekly plan based on your needs.

Suppose you are given a prompt :

You: “Create a 5-day best interview preparation plan for a professional who is doing an entry-level software engineering job.”

ChatGPT: “Sure! Here is a list to help you out

Day 1: Resume + HR Questionnaire

Day 2: DSA Basics category questions

Day 3: System Design Basics, logic

Day 4: Mock Interviews preparation

Day 5: Behavioral Questions + Final Review rounds”

Apart from this, it will also give you daily tasks and milestones that will help you stay on track.

Bonus question that you can ask :

Ask about the particular Company or even some interesting Industry Trends.

Do you want to impress your interviewer? Show them what you know about the particular company, the competition they have, and the latest interesting industry trends available.

You can also ask ChatGPT some of the following questions :

  • “What is the latest news about XYZ company trending in the market?”
  • “What is the work culture and employees' review at Goldman Sachs?”
  • “Explain the impact of GenAI on the best latest marketing job roles offered.”
