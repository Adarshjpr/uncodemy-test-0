What is ChatGPT, and why should digital marketers in particular care about it?

Let's start with some of the basics. This ChatGPT is one of the advanced AI language models that is developed by OpenAI. They are also designed to understand as well as generate some human-like text.

Why does all this matter to digital marketers

Marketing is simply all about communication - creating content, audience interaction, and customer engagement are areas where ChatGPT excels a lot.

It's simply like having :

  • A strategist
  • A copywriter
  • A data assistant
  • And also a social media manager, everything is rolled out into one virtual assistant that will never sleep.

Benefits of ChatGPT for the Digital Marketplace

Let's dive into some top ways why digital marketers are using ChatGPT to streamline a set of operations, workflows, boost productivity, and also be ahead in the competition.

  1. Content creation at a scale

    2. Creating some high-quality content consistently can be quite an exhausting task. With ChatGPT, it is easier than ever to :

    • Write product descriptions as well as category content with ease.

    Pro tip: Just feed ChatGPT your brand tone and also buyer persona, just for an even more tailored set of content.

  2. Keyword integration & SEO optimization

    3. Do you wish to rank higher on the Google search engine?

    In this scenario, ChatGPT can just help you

    • Generating SEO optimised blog titles.
    • Crafting some meta titles and descriptions.
    • For better keyword placement, you can rewrite the content.
    • Suggest great long-tail Keywords which are based on the niche.

    Example :

    An affiliate marketer used ChatGPT to help them rewrite a 1500-word blog post. This is done as they will help with better keyword density and also get about a 35% traffic boost in two weeks.

  3. Social media strategy and scheduling ideas.

    4. Are you struggling to come up with some exclusive content for your different social channels? ?.So, ChatGPT is just your brainstorming buddy at that time.

    It can just :

    • Suggest some content calendars.
    • Create some poll questions, carousel ideas, and even some content scripts.
    • You can also write captivating captions with emojis, hashtags, and CTAs.

    Example

    A digital agency will use ChatGPT to generate about 30 days of LinkedIn content for its B2B SaaS clients, which will ultimately reduce planning time.

  4. Email marketing campaigns

    5. Do you wish for better open rates and also click-throughs?

    Let this ChatGPT help you draft some irresistible email content.

    It can also be written as mentioned below :

    • Subject lines that will increase open rates to a huge extent.
    • Some persuasive copy for sales funnels.
    • Welcome emails, event invitations, and some thank you notes
    • Follow-up emails and lead-nurturing sequences.

    Example :

    An e-commerce brand used ChatGPT to rewrite their abandoned cart email sequence and saw about a 20% increase in their recovery rates.

  5. Ad copy & A/B testing ideas

    6. Writing ads that will convert is an art as well as a science. ChatGPT will make it easier to :

    • Create multiple variations of a headline.
    • Fine-tune interesting CTAs based on your campaign goals.
    • Suggest both split tests as well as creatives.
    • Adapt to demographics and also platforms.

    Example :

    A marketing consultant fed data on different customer behaviours to ChatGPT. So it just generated different Facebook ads with CTRs that will exceed previous benchmarks by about 25.

  6. Chatbots and customer support

    7. You can integrate ChatGPT into websites or some messaging platforms to :

    • Answer some common FAQs
    • Assist with some product recommendations
    • Provide some live support scripts
    • Guide users through troubleshooting platforms.

    Example:

    A SaaS company has built a chatbot-powered chatbot on their particular landing page that will reduce support ticket volume by 40%.

  7. Market research & competitor analysis

    8. Not sure what is trending or even how your competitors are positioning themselves.

    Use ChatGPT for the following?

    • Summarizing interesting industry trends.
    • Create a template for SWOT analysis.
    • Generate real real-life customer persona.
    • Extract some insights from competitor websites or even the reviews.

    Example

    A startup has used ChatGPT to analyse user reviews. This is done across multiple competitors, and key gaps are identified so that they can easily fill in their product map.

How to use Chaeffectively in your Marketing workflows, particularly.

Here are some interesting ways in which you can implement ChatGPT in your daily set of tasks :

Try to use prompts strategically and effectively:

Instead of saying things like “Write a blog.”

You can write :

“Write a 1000-word blog post having a professional tone about how small businesses can just use Instagram reels for lead generation purposes.”

The more specific prompt you will give the better will be your outcome also in the meanwhile.

Set up some brand voice guidelines.

Before diving deep in, we need to provide ChatGPT with :

  • Ideal customer profile.
  • Common types of phrases or industry terms that you have ever used.
  • Your brand’s tone ( professional, witty, or even friendly )

Never forget to use ChatGPT as a collaborator and not a replacement.

You should never rely 100% on AI. You should treat them as a creative partner. So with this, you can overcome writer's block, speed up some drafts, or even generate some interesting variations for testing purposes.

Solution: Always just fact-check and also tweak outputs that will reflect your brand for sure.
