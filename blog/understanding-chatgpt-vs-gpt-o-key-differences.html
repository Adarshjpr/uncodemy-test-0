Understanding ChatGPT vs GPT-4o: Key Differences

  • Mr. Irsad 3 days ago
  • 15
  • 11 min read

What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a chatbot that is developed by OpenAI. It is also designed to generate human-like text based on all the prompts that it just receives. It is just one of the conversational models that is powered by GPT language models. All of them start from GPT 3.5 - GPT 4.

This ChatGPT became quite a global sensation because of its ability to mimic natural language, offer some of the best helpful answers, generate some interesting content, write code as well as pass in exams.

Since its public release it has been integrated into various industries. All this ranges from customer support and education to marketing and other content creation stuff.

But the main thing is there - ChatGPT is not a single model at all. It is just an interface that uses other different underlying models. All this includes GPT - 3.5, GPT - 4 and now the GPT - 4o. So whenever people say ChatGPT they might mean that tool and not necessarily the latest model on which it runs.

What is GPT - 4o ?

In GPT - 4o the o present over there stands for omni. This is one of the new models of OpenAI that was launched in 2024. It is built to be a multimodal one which often means it can understand and also generate :

  • Text
  • Images
  • Video ( to an extent it is just the future )
  • Audio

Unlike its best predecessor we can see that GPT - 4o is trained natively to process different multiple modes of communication just at one glance. You can just speak to it, show it something, type it out and you will get a response in remarkable naturalness.All this just represents a major transformational step towards real time and some intuitive human AI interactions

Some of the core differences between ChatGPT and GPT - 4o

  1. ChatGPT is just a platform while GPT - 4o is a model

    2. You can think of it like ChatGPT as the house and GPT - 4o is often one of the brains inside it.

    • This ChatGPT is often the chat interface on other platforms like even OpenAI websites or just the app.
    • It just makes it possible for users to interact with GPT -3.5, GPT -4 and GPT - 4o.
    • GPT - 4o is one of the most recent and also advanced models that you can use within ChatGPT.
  2. Multimodal capability - In this GPT -4o is just omni powered

    3. This is just where GPT - 4o will already shine.

    FeaturesChatGPT with 3.5/4GPT - 4o
    Text✓✓

    Image inputX ( limited )✓
    Voice inputX ( Added later with set of delays )✓( native )

  3. Speed as well as latency

    4. If you might have used GPT - 4 then you must have noticed that it is a bit slow. This is mainly when one has to generate large responses.

    GPT - 4o is comparatively faster. It will not only generate text faster but its voice mode will just respond in 300 milliseconds. This is quite similar to how humans converse

    Low latency will open up possibilities for different real time applications like live language translation, voice based tutoring and also some accessibility based tools.

  4. Cost and access

    5. This OpenAI just offers ChatGPT in both free as well as paid versions.

    PlanAvailable modelMonthly price

    The version of GPT - 3.5 is still available free of cost to users but multimodal capabilities are lacking in that.

    Bottom line for this - only in the paid tier GPT - 4o is available. But all this will significantly offer some more features and smart output.

  5. Intelligence and also reasoning abilities

    6. This GPT - 4o is not just about modes and speed but it is a quite smarter one.

    According to the OpenAI GPT - 5o is known for performing pn par with GPT - 4 in different benchmark tests. All these tests include ( SATs, var exam, Olympiad level exam etc) but all this is done with better memory and also handling of mixed inputs.

    It is also quite better at :

    • Solving some logic puzzles
    • Understanding context in deep manner
    • Interpreting complex set of instructions
    • Following some nuanced conversational tone.

    You can now just for example

    • Speak to it quite naturally and also have it remember your tone.
    • Share a screenshot of an error or code and tell it to debug that code.
    • Show GPT - 4o a math problem related image and later ask for a solution.
  6. Voice as well as emotion

    7. One of the quite futuristic features of this GPT - 4o is the ability to understand and express emotions through speech.

    It can just :

    • Adjust both tone and inflection on real time basis
    • Help with language learning by mimicking accents or even correcting pronunciation.
    • Detect emotions in your particular voice and then respond empathically towards it.

    All these features just make GPT - 4o feel like a pro bot and also more like a conversational companion anytime.

  7. Memory as well as context length

    8. Another major improvement in GPT - 4o is the context window. This just contains the amount of information that it can return during a conversation.

    • GPT - 4o just supports about 128,000 tokens. This roughly means 300 pages of text.
    • GPT 3.5 will support only 4000 tokens.

    This simply means GPT - 4o can hold longer conversations, remember all the minute details that you feed and also process large documents without even forgetting large earlier parts of dialogue.

    Memory features are also introduced by OpenAI just allowing GPT - 4o to just recall preference, name as well.as past chats if enabled also.

Some use cases of GPT-4o

Here are some of the interesting ways in which this GPT -4o is already used.

  • Live language translation in a particular conversation.
  • Visual analysis for describing photos and also solving problems
  • Mental health companions with emotional intelligence are also there to help you out
  • Debugging of code from the provided screenshots.
  • Accessibility tools for both visually and hearing impaired students.

Should you upgrade to GPT - 4o ?

If you are a casual user who just types simple queries then GPay - 3.5 is more than sufficient for you.

However, if you are someone who :

  • Requires help with a complex set of tasks
  • Works frequently with code, images or even documents.
  • Values a more natural type of human like AI.
  • Needs faster as well as more accurate response ls.
  • If you enjoy speaking to your assistant rather than typing it.

In all above scenarios GPT - 4o is quite worthy a penny.

Future beyond GPT-4o

This GPT - 5o has been providing a major leap but it is not the end at all. It is just paving the way for ChatGPT and also beyond. Below AI could :

  • Handle real world robotic set of instructions
  • Become a deeply personalized set of assistants.
  • Through video feeds one can also perceive the world.
  • You can also make them integrated with cars, wearables and also smart home.

Nowadays we are entering a phase where AI is becoming intuitive and also ambient it just means something that works for you and not just with you.

The difference among two is not just about technology it is just about how we connect with machines.
