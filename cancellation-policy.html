Our Cancellation & Refund Policy

Transparent, fair, and customer-focused policies for your peace of mind

Cancellation Policy

  • Once a payment is successfully completed, cancellation of the order is not permitted.
  • Enrolment in any Uncodemy course, program, service, or subscription is considered final and non-cancellable.
  • Access to learning resources, mentorship, software tools, and support services is granted immediately after payment; therefore, cancellations cannot be accommodated.

Refund Policy

No refunds shall be provided for:

  • Online or offline training programs
  • Digital courses, workshops, or bootcamps
  • Subscription-based services
  • Mentorship, placement assistance, or consulting services

Refund requests will only be considered in exceptional cases, such as:

  • Duplicate payment
  • Technical error resulting in failed service access

Such requests must be raised within 48 hours of the transaction.

Refund Processing Timeline

  • If a refund is approved after internal verification, it will be processed within 7–10 business working days.
  • The refunded amount will be credited back to the original payment method.
  • Uncodemy is not responsible for delays caused by banks or payment gateways.

EMI Transactions

For payments made using Credit Card EMI (including HDFC Bank EMI):

  • Once the EMI transaction is completed, cancellation or refund is strictly not allowed.
  • EMI conversion, interest charges, tenure, and billing are governed by the issuing bank's policies.
  • Any disputes related to EMI schedules or charges must be addressed directly with the respective bank.

Payment Gateway Disclaimer

  • Uncodemy uses trusted third-party payment gateways such as Razorpay.
  • Uncodemy shall not be held liable for transaction failures, technical errors, or delays caused by payment gateway providers or banking institutions.
