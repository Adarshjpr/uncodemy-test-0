💼 Live Industrial Projects

🛒 Retail Domain

1. Customer Segmentation

Objective: Focuses on grouping customers into distinct segments based on their behavior, preferences, and purchasing patterns. These insights help businesses design personalized marketing campaigns to better meet customer needs and boost engagement.

Approach: Group customers based on purchasing behaviors.

Execution: Analyze customer demographics and purchase history. Use K-Means to form clusters. Visualize results in Tableau.

Programming & Tools: Python (K-Means Clustering), Tableau.

🛍️ E-Commerce

2. Pricing Strategy Optimization

Objective: Analyze pricing strategies to maximize revenue.

Approach: Use historical pricing and sales data to find optimal prices. Get data from E-commerce platform. Reporting and sharing.

Execution: Analyze price elasticity of products. Build a demand curve model. Visualize results and recommendations.

Programming & Tools: Python (Pandas, NumPy), Tableau.

👔 Human Resources

3. Employee Performance Analysis

Objective: Analyzing employee performance data to identify top performers, areas of improvement, and training needs. By using metrics like productivity, efficiency, and feedback scores, the analysis supports fair appraisals and helps design targeted development programs to enhance overall workforce performance.

Approach: Evaluate KPIs and identify areas of improvement. Share them with stakeholders also create an automation to track time to time improvement in performance.

Execution: Import performance data into Excel. Calculate KPIs and identify top/bottom performers. Visualize trends in Tableau.

Programming & Tools: Excel, Tableau/Power BI.

📢 Advertising

4. Marketing Campaign Analysis

Objective: Evaluate the success of marketing campaigns.

Approach: Measure ROI and customer engagement.

Execution: Extract data on campaign performance. Use SQL to analyze key metrics like CTR and conversion rate. Visualize campaign results in Tableau.

Programming & Tools: Excel, Tableau, SQL.

📞 Customer Service

5. Streamlining Customer Support

Objective: Improve operational efficiency in customer support by analyzing existing workflows, identifying bottlenecks, and recommending tools and processes to enhance response times & customer satisfaction.

Approach: Map the current customer support workflow. Identify bottlenecks such as manual tasks causing delays. Analyze customer satisfaction data to pinpoint improvement areas. Design a new workflow incorporating tools like chatbots and e-ticketing systems. Recommend KPIs to track and measure performance improvements.

Execution: Use BPMN tools to map the current customer support process, focusing on identifying manual steps, delays, and inefficiencies. Collect data on key metrics like response times, resolution times, and customer satisfaction scores, organizing it in Excel for analysis. Highlight bottlenecks such as manual ticket assignments that cause delays or errors.

Programming & Tools: Process Mapping (BPMN), Excel, JIRA.

🛍️ E-Commerce

6. Inventory Management

Objective: Optimize inventory levels by analyzing sales trends and demand patterns. The goal is to minimize costs while ensuring the right products are always available to meet customer needs.

Approach: We analyze inventory turnover rates to understand how quickly products are sold and restocked. Additionally, we determine optimal reorder levels to ensure stock availability minimizing storage costs.

Execution: Analyze historical inventory data. Calculate optimal reorder points using Python. Summarize findings in Excel reports.

Programming & Tools: Python, Excel, Power BI.
