❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find comprehensive answers to common questions about our masterclasses, registration process, certificates, resources, and learning experience. Still have questions? Contact our support team.

Are the masterclasses really completely free with no hidden charges? ▼
Yes, absolutely! All our masterclasses, workshops, and webinars are 100% free with zero hidden charges, no subscription fees, and no payment requirements. You get free access to live sessions, comprehensive study materials, project files, certificates, community support, session recordings, and lifetime resources. Our mission is to democratize quality tech education and make it accessible to everyone regardless of financial background or location.
Will I receive a certificate after attending the masterclass? ▼
Yes! Every participant who attends the full masterclass session receives a professional digital certificate of participation. The certificate includes your name, workshop title, date, duration, instructor signature, Uncodemy logo, and a unique verification ID for credential verification. You can download it immediately after the session, add it to your LinkedIn profile, share it on social media, include it in your resume, and showcase your continuous learning to potential employers.
What if I miss a live session or cannot attend at scheduled time? ▼
Don't worry! We understand that schedules can be challenging and time zones vary. All masterclass sessions are professionally recorded and made available to all registered participants within 24 hours via email. You can access the complete recording through your registered email, watch at your convenience, pause and replay sections, and still download all study materials and resources. However, to receive the participation certificate, you need to either attend the live session or complete a short assignment based on the recorded content.
Do I need prior programming knowledge or technical background? ▼
It depends on the specific masterclass level. We clearly mark each event as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced in the event details. Beginner-level sessions require absolutely no prior knowledge and start from fundamental concepts. Intermediate sessions expect basic programming familiarity and understanding of core concepts. Advanced workshops require specific pre-requisites which are mentioned in the event description. Always check the event details, prerequisites, and recommended background before registering to ensure the best learning experience.
Can I interact with instructors and ask questions during sessions? ▼
Absolutely! Our masterclasses are highly interactive and designed for maximum engagement. You can ask questions through live chat, participate in polls and quizzes, join breakout rooms for group discussions, raise your hand for voice questions, share your screen for code reviews, and get real-time doubt resolution from expert instructors. We encourage active participation as it significantly enhances learning outcomes, networking opportunities, and overall experience. The Q&A sessions are specifically designed for personalized guidance and clarification.
How long are the masterclass sessions typically? ▼
Most masterclasses range from 2 to 3 hours, including theory explanation, practical demonstrations, live coding sessions, hands-on exercises, project work, and comprehensive Q&A sessions. We design sessions to be thorough yet concise, focusing on high-impact learning without overwhelming participants. Some specialized workshops may extend to 4 hours with scheduled breaks included for optimal learning experience, retention, and participant comfort. The exact duration is clearly mentioned in each event description.
Will I get study materials, code files, and additional resources? ▼
Yes! All registered participants receive comprehensive study materials including presentation slides, complete code repositories on GitHub, practice exercises with solutions, reference documentation, e-books, cheat sheets, tool recommendations, resource links, project templates, and additional learning materials. These resources are shared via email after the session and remain accessible forever. You can revisit them anytime to reinforce your learning, practice concepts, build projects, and continue your skill development journey.

Absolutely! There's no limit to how many masterclasses you can attend. We encourage continuous learning and skill development across multiple domains.
