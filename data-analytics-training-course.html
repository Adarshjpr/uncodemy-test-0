What is Data Analytics?

Data analytics involves examining raw data to uncover patterns, draw conclusions, and support decision-making. It blends statistical analysis, machine learning, and data visualization to turn data into actionable insights. Analysts work with tools such as Excel, SQL, Python, R, Tableau, and Power BI to clean, process, and interpret data across industries.

In essence, data analytics enables professionals to predict trends, optimize performance, improve customer experiences, and drive better business outcomes. With the rise of big data and automation, skilled analysts are now indispensable across sectors like finance, retail, technology, logistics, and more.
