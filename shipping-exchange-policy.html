Shipping & Exchange Policy

At Uncodemy, we primarily offer digital services and online/offline training programs. Please review the policy below to understand how service delivery and exchanges are handled.

1. Service Delivery (Shipping)

  • Uncodemy does not ship any physical products.
  • All courses, training programs, learning materials, and services are delivered in digital or in-person format.
  • Access credentials, course links, or onboarding details are shared via email, WhatsApp, or the registered student portal within 24–48 working hours after successful payment confirmation.
  • In case of offline or classroom training, joining instructions are communicated separately after enrollment.

2. Exchange Policy

  • Since Uncodemy provides intangible digital services, exchange of services is not applicable.
  • Once a learner is enrolled in a specific course or program, it cannot be exchanged for another course, batch, or service.
  • Any request for changes will be reviewed at Uncodemy's discretion and is not guaranteed.

3. Delayed or Failed Access

  • If a customer does not receive course access or onboarding details within the specified time, they must notify Uncodemy within 48 hours of payment.
  • Our support team will resolve access-related issues on priority.

4. Third-Party Tools & Platforms

  • Some courses may involve access to third-party tools, platforms, or software.
  • Uncodemy is not responsible for delays or issues caused by third-party service providers.
